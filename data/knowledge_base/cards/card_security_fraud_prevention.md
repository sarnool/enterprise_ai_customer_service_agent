## Card Security Features

### Chip-and-PIN Technology
- EMV chip embedded in all cards
- Dynamic authentication for each transaction
- PIN required for transactions above ₹5,000
- More secure than magnetic stripe

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- OTP sent to registered mobile for online transactions
- Additional password for internet banking linked transactions
- Biometric authentication available on compatible devices

### Tokenization
- Card details replaced with unique tokens for online payments
- Tokens used for recurring payments (subscriptions, EMIs)
- Actual card number never shared with merchants

### 3D Secure
- Verified by Visa / Mastercard SecureCode
- Additional authentication layer for online transactions
- Mandatory for all e-commerce transactions

## Fraud Prevention Guidelines

### Protect Your PIN
- **Never** share your PIN with anyone, including bank staff
- **Never** write PIN on card or keep it in wallet
- Shield keypad while entering PIN at ATM/POS
- Change PIN regularly (every 3-6 months recommended)
- Use unique PIN, not birthdate or phone number

### Safe Card Usage
- Keep card in sight during POS transactions
- Don't allow merchants to take card out of view
- Sign on the back of card immediately upon receipt
- Never lend your card to others
- Check card is returned after every transaction

### Online Transaction Safety
- Shop only on secure websites (https://, padlock symbol)
- Avoid saving card details on merchant websites
- Never share OTP or CVV with anyone
- Don't use public Wi-Fi for transactions
- Enable transaction alerts

### ATM Safety
- Use ATMs in well-lit, populated areas
- Check for skimming devices before inserting card
- Shield PIN entry from cameras and onlookers
- Cancel transaction if anything seems suspicious
- Don't accept help from strangers at ATM

## Common Fraud Types and Prevention

### 1. Card Skimming

**What is it:**
- Devices installed on ATMs to steal card data
- Captures magnetic stripe information
- PIN captured via hidden cameras or fake keypads

**Prevention:**
- Inspect ATM for unusual devices
- Wiggle card slot to check if loose
- Cover PIN entry with hand
- Prefer ATMs inside bank branches

**Red Flags:**
- Loose or unusual-looking card reader
- Extra camera pointed at keypad
- Keypad feels thick or different

---

### 2. Phishing (Email/SMS)

**What is it:**
- Fake emails/SMS claiming to be from bank
- Asks for card details, PIN, OTP, password
- Links to fake websites

**Prevention:**
- Bank never asks for PIN/CVV/OTP via email or SMS
- Don't click links in suspicious emails
- Verify sender email address
- Type bank URL directly in browser

**Example Phishing Messages:**
- "Your card is blocked, click here to unblock"
- "Update your KYC to avoid account closure"
- "You've won a prize, enter card details to claim"

---

### 3. Vishing (Phone Calls)

**What is it:**
- Fraudsters call pretending to be bank staff
- Urgency tactics to get card details
- May ask for OTP or remote access to phone

**Prevention:**
- Bank never asks for full card number or PIN on call
- Disconnect and call official customer care number
- Don't share OTP received during the call
- Never install apps as directed by caller

**Warning Signs:**
- Caller claims card will be blocked urgently
- Asks for CVV or PIN
- Requests remote access to phone/computer

---

### 4. Card Not Present (CNP) Fraud

**What is it:**
- Unauthorized online transactions using stolen card details
- Card details obtained through data breaches or phishing

**Prevention:**
- Enable transaction alerts
- Review statements regularly
- Use virtual card numbers for online shopping
- Set conservative online transaction limits
- Disable international transactions when not needed

---

### 5. Lost/Stolen Card Misuse

**What is it:**
- Physical card used by unauthorized person
- Contactless payments don't require PIN (up to ₹5,000)

**Prevention:**
- Report lost card immediately
- Block card via mobile app instantly
- Set low contactless transaction limits
- Enable location-based transaction alerts

## Zero Liability Protection

### Customer Protection Policy

**Zero Liability Conditions:**
- Unauthorized transactions reported within 24 hours
- Customer not negligent (didn't share PIN/OTP)
- Card blocked immediately upon discovery

**Limited Liability:**
- Report within 3 days: Maximum liability ₹10,000
- Report within 7 days: Maximum liability ₹25,000
- Report after 7 days: Liability as per investigation

**Customer Negligence (No Protection):**
- Sharing PIN or OTP voluntarily
- Delayed reporting without valid reason
- Gross negligence in safeguarding card

## Transaction Monitoring

### Bank's Fraud Detection System

**Real-Time Monitoring:**
- AI-powered fraud detection
- Unusual transaction patterns flagged
- High-risk merchant category alerts
- Geographic anomaly detection (e.g., transaction in two cities simultaneously)

**Automated Blocks:**
- Card temporarily blocked if suspicious activity detected
- SMS sent to customer for verification
- Unblocked after customer confirms legitimacy

**Risk Scoring:**
- Each transaction assigned risk score
- High-risk transactions require additional authentication
- Declined if risk exceeds threshold

## Reporting Fraud

### Immediate Actions

**Step 1: Block the Card**
- Call 24/7 helpline: 1800-XXX-CARD
- Block via mobile app (instant)
- Block via internet banking
- Visit nearest branch

**Step 2: Report Fraud**
- File complaint via customer care
- Register FIR at police station (for large amounts)
- Note complaint reference number

**Step 3: Dispute Transaction**
- Provide transaction details (date, amount, merchant)
- Submit written complaint within 7 days
- Provide supporting documents if any

### Investigation Process
1. Complaint registered (reference number issued)
2. Transaction analysis (7-15 days)
3. Merchant investigation if needed
4. Decision communicated to customer
5. Refund processed if fraud confirmed (30-45 days)

## What Bank Will Never Ask

**Bank will NEVER ask for:**
- Complete card number (may ask last 4 digits for verification)
- CVV (3-digit code on back of card)
- PIN (never, under any circumstance)
- OTP received on your phone
- Internet banking password
- Debit card expiry date (in combination with other details)

**If anyone asks for these, it's a scam. Disconnect immediately.**
