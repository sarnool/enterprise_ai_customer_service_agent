## Overview

Our reward programs are designed to thank you for using your credit and debit cards. Earn points, miles, or cashback on every transaction and redeem them for exciting rewards, travel, shopping vouchers, and more.

## Credit Card Reward Programs

### Classic Credit Card Rewards

**Earning Structure:**
- 1 reward point per ₹150 spent on retail purchases
- No points on fuel, utility bills, wallet loads, cash advances
- Bonus 500 points on card anniversary (if annual spends > ₹1 lakh)

**Point Value:** 1 point = ₹0.25

**Validity:** 2 years from earning date

**Redemption Options:**
- Shopping vouchers (Amazon, Flipkart, etc.)
- Travel bookings
- Statement credit (minimum 2,000 points)
- Merchandise from reward catalog

**Minimum Redemption:** 500 points

---

### Miles Earning

**Base Earning:**
- 1 mile per ₹1 spent on flight and hotel bookings
- 0.5 miles per ₹1 on all other purchases
- 3 miles per ₹1 on partner airline bookings

**Bonus Miles:**

**Other Redemptions:**
- Rental car bookings

## Cashback Credit Card Benefits

### Cashback Structure

**Online Shopping:** 5% cashback (maximum ₹500 per month)
**Dining:** 2% cashback (no cap)
**Retail:** 1% cashback (all other purchases)
**Fuel:** 1% surcharge waiver

**Cashback Credit:**
- Credited to card account at end of each billing cycle
- Automatically adjusts against next bill
- No minimum earning, no expiry
- Reflected as statement credit

**Example:**
- Spend ₹10,000 online shopping = ₹500 cashback
- Spend ₹5,000 dining = ₹100 cashback
- Spend ₹20,000 other retail = ₹200 cashback
- **Total monthly cashback: ₹800** (adjusted in next bill)

- Points earned on eligible transactions only
- Reversed transactions: Points debited back
- Fraudulent/disputed transactions: No points
- Cash advances, balance transfers: No points
- Points non-transferable (except family pooling for premium cards)

**Expiry Notification:**
- SMS/Email sent 30 days before expiry

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I combine points from multiple cards?**
A: Family points pooling available for Platinum cards only. Primary and add-on cards automatically pool.

**Q: Do I earn points on EMI transactions?**
A: Yes, points earned at time of purchase (before EMI conversion). No additional points on EMI installments.

**Q: Can I gift my points to someone?**
A: No, points are non-transferable to non-family members.

**Q: What happens to points if I close my card?**
A: Points forfeited upon card closure. Redeem before closing.

**Q: How do I check my points balance?**
A : Via mobile app, internet banking, monthly statement, or call customer care.

**Q: Can I choose not to participate in the rewards program?**
A: Participation is automatic and cannot be opted out.
