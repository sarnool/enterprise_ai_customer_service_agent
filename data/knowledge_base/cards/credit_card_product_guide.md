## Credit Card Variants

### 1. Classic Credit Card

**Target Customers:** First-time credit card users, salaried individuals

**Annual Fee:** ₹500 (waived if annual spends > ₹1 lakh)

**Key Features:**
- Credit limit: ₹50,000 - ₹2 lakh
- Interest-free credit period: Up to 50 days
- Cash withdrawal limit: 40% of credit limit
- Contactless payment enabled
- Zero liability on lost card (if reported within 24 hours)

**Reward Points:**
- 1 point per ₹150 spent on retail purchases
- No points on fuel, utility bills, or cash withdrawals
- Points valid for 2 years

**Eligibility:**
- Minimum age: 21 years
- Minimum income: ₹3 lakh per annum
- Good credit score (> 700)

---

### 2. Gold Credit Card

**Target Customers:** Premium customers, frequent travelers

**Annual Fee:** ₹2,500 (waived if annual spends > ₹3 lakh)

**Key Features:**
- Credit limit: ₹2 lakh - ₹10 lakh
- Interest-free credit period: Up to 50 days
- Cash withdrawal limit: 50% of credit limit
- Complimentary airport lounge access: 4 visits per year
- Travel insurance: ₹50 lakh coverage
- Personal accident insurance: ₹10 lakh

**Reward Points:**
- 2 points per ₹150 spent on retail
- 5 points per ₹150 on dining and travel
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver (₹250 per transaction)
- Points valid for 3 years

**Eligibility:**
- Minimum age: 21 years
- Minimum income: ₹6 lakh per annum
- Excellent credit score (> 750)

---

- Cash withdrawal limit: 50% of credit limit

**Reward Points:**
- 3 points per ₹150 spent on retail
- 10 points per ₹150 on international transactions
- 5 points per ₹150 on dining, travel, entertainment
- Complimentary Priority Pass membership
- Points never expire

**Eligibility:**
- Minimum age: 21 years
- Minimum income: ₹12 lakh per annum
- Excellent credit score (> 800)

### 4. Travel Rewards Card

**Target Customers:** Frequent flyers, international travelers

**Annual Fee:** ₹3,000 (2,500 bonus miles on card activation)

**Key Features:**
- Credit limit: ₹3 lakh - ₹15 lakh

- No foreign transaction fee (FX markup: 1.5% only)
- Complimentary travel insurance
- Airport lounge access: Unlimited

**Mile Redemption:**
- Miles can be transferred to partner airlines
- Book flights directly using miles
- Miles valid for 5 years

**Eligibility:**
- Minimum age: 21 years
- Minimum income: ₹8 lakh per annum
- Frequent international travel history preferred

### 5. Cashback Credit Card

**Target Customers:** Budget-conscious users, heavy online shoppers

**Annual Fee:** ₹1,000 (waived if annual spends > ₹2 lakh)

**Key Features:**
- Credit limit: ₹1 lakh - ₹5 lakh

**Eligibility:**

- Minimum income: ₹4 lakh per annum
- Good credit score (> 700)

## Interest Rates and Charges

### Interest on Outstanding Balance
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR): 36% - 42% per annum
- Monthly interest rate: 3% - 3.5%
- Interest charged on daily outstanding balance
- No interest if full payment made by due date

### Late Payment Charges
- Up to ₹1,000 outstanding: ₹400
- ₹1,000 - ₹10,000: ₹750
- ₹10,000 - ₹25,000: ₹950
- Above ₹25,000: ₹1,200

### Other Charges
- Cash advance fee: 2.5% of transaction amount (minimum ₹300)
- Over-limit penalty: ₹500 per instance
- Foreign currency markup: 3.5% (1.5% for Travel Card)
- Card replacement: ₹100
- Dynamic currency conversion: Additional 2%

---

## Repayment Options

### Minimum Amount Due
- 5% of outstanding balance or ₹500 (whichever is higher)
- Plus any overdue amount from previous months
- Interest charged on remaining balance

### Payment Methods
- Online payment via internet/mobile banking
- Auto-debit from savings account
- Cash/cheque at branch
- NEFT/RTGS transfer
- UPI payment

### EMI Conversion
- Available for purchases above ₹5,000
- Tenure: 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 months
- Processing fee: ₹199 per EMI conversion
- Interest rate: 12-18% per annum

## Credit Limit Enhancement

**Eligibility for Increase:**
- Card active for minimum 6 months
- Good payment history (no late payments)
- Credit utilization < 50%
- Income increase documentation

**Process:**
- Submit limit enhancement request online or at branch
- Credit bureau check performed
- Decision communicated within 7 working days
- Enhancement can be up to 50% of current limit

## Application Process

**Documents Required:**
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport)
- Address proof (utility bill, Aadhaar)
- Income proof (salary slips, ITR, Form 16)
- Bank statements (last 3 months)
- Photograph

**Processing Time:** 7-10 working days

**Approval Factors:**
- Credit score
- Income stability
- Employment type
- Existing debt obligations
- Banking relationship

---

## Responsible Credit Usage

**Best Practices:**
- Pay full outstanding amount by due date
- Maintain credit utilization below 30%
- Set up auto-pay for minimum amount
- Review monthly statements
- Report unauthorized transactions immediately

**Warning Signs:**
- Only paying minimum due repeatedly
- Using cash withdrawal frequently
- Approaching credit limit
- Missing payment due dates
