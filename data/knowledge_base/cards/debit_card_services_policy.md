## Overview

Debit cards provide convenient access to your savings or current account funds for purchases, withdrawals, and online transactions. This policy outlines debit card variants, features, transaction limits, and usage guidelines.

---

## ATM Services

### Cash Withdrawal
- Available 24/7 at all bank ATMs and partner ATMs
- Own bank ATMs: Unlimited free transactions
- Other bank ATMs: 5 free per month, then ₹20 per transaction (metro), ₹10 (non-metro)

### Balance Inquiry
- Free at own bank ATMs
- Other bank ATMs: Free (included in 5 free transactions)

### Mini Statement
- Last 5 transactions displayed
- Free at all ATMs

### PIN Change
- Can be changed at any ATM
- Free service
- New PIN effective immediately

---

## Point of Sale (POS) Transactions

### Merchant Payments
- Swipe and pay at retail outlets, restaurants, fuel stations
- Pay using contactless (tap to pay)
- Enter 4-digit PIN for transactions above ₹5,000
- No charges for POS transactions

### Transaction Security
- Chip-and-PIN technology
- Two-factor authentication for online transactions
- Real-time SMS/email alerts
- Option to block/unblock card via mobile app

---

## Online Transactions

### E-commerce Purchases
- Accepted at all major online platforms
- 3D Secure (OTP-based authentication) for enhanced security
- Virtual Card Number (VCN) option available for added security

**Daily Limits (can be customized):**
- Classic Card: ₹50,000
- Gold Card: ₹1,00,000
- Platinum Card: ₹2,00,000

### International Online Transactions
- Enable/disable international usage via mobile app or internet banking
- Foreign currency markup: 3.5% on transaction value
- Dynamic Currency Conversion available (additional charges apply)

## Card Replacement

### Reasons for Replacement
- Lost or stolen card
- Damaged or malfunctioning card
- Card expiry
- Upgrade to higher variant

**Process:**
1. Report lost/stolen card immediately via helpline or app
2. Request replacement through internet banking, mobile app, or branch
3. Old card blocked immediately
4. New card delivered within 5 working days
5. New PIN sent separately

**Charges:**
- Lost/Stolen: ₹100
- Damaged: ₹50
- Expiry renewal: Free
- Upgrade: As per new card fee

## International Usage

**Activation Required:**
- International usage disabled by default
- Enable via mobile app, internet banking, or customer care
- Can be enabled for specific duration (temporary activation)

**Accepted Networks:**
- Visa/Mastercard merchant locations worldwide
- International ATM networks (Visa Plus, Mastercard Cirrus)

**Currency Conversion:**
- Charged in local currency and converted to INR
- Exchange rate as per Visa/Mastercard rates
- 3.5% foreign currency markup applied

**Travel Tips:**
- Inform bank before international travel
- Carry backup card
- Keep emergency contact numbers handy
- Check daily limits before travel

## Dispute Resolution

### Unauthorized Transactions
- Report within 24 hours for zero liability
- Report within 3 days for limited liability (max ₹10,000)
- Report after 3 days: Customer liable for losses until reported

**Dispute Process:**
1. Call customer care immediately
2. Block card to prevent further misuse
3. File formal complaint with transaction details
4. Investigation completed within 15 working days
5. Refund processed if fraud confirmed

## Contactless Payments

### How It Works
- Tap card on POS terminal for payments up to ₹5,000
- No PIN required for transactions below ₹5,000
- Fast and convenient for small purchases

**Security:**
- Limited transaction amount
- Can be disabled via mobile app
- Alerts for every contactless transaction
