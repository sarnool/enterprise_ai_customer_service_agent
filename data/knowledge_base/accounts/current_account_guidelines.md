## Overview

Current accounts are non-interest bearing deposit accounts designed for businesses, professionals, and institutions requiring frequent transactions. This document outlines eligibility, features, transaction limits, and operational guidelines.

---

## Current Account Variants

### 1. Basic Current Account

**Target Customers:** Small businesses, sole proprietors, freelancers

**Key Features:**
- Minimum balance requirement: ₹10,000
- Free cash deposits: Up to ₹1 lakh per month
- Free cheque book: 50 leaves per quarter
- Online banking with bulk payment facility
- Free demand drafts: 5 per quarter
- SMS and email alerts

**Transaction Limits:**
- Daily cash withdrawal: ₹1 lakh
- Monthly free transactions: 100 (beyond this: ₹5 per transaction)

**Fee Structure:**
- Account opening: ₹1,000
- Annual maintenance: ₹5,000
- Minimum balance penalty: ₹500 per quarter

---

### 2. Premium Current Account

**Target Customers:** Medium and large businesses, corporate clients

**Key Features:**
- Minimum balance requirement: ₹1,00,000
- Free cash deposits: Up to ₹10 lakh per month
- Unlimited free cheque leaves
- Dedicated relationship manager
- Priority transaction processing
- Trade finance services
- Forex services
- Corporate credit card linkage

**Transaction Limits:**
- Daily cash withdrawal: ₹5 lakh (can be enhanced)
- Unlimited free transactions

**Fee Structure:**
- Account opening: ₹5,000
- Annual maintenance: ₹25,000 (waived if average balance > ₹5 lakh)
- No minimum balance penalty if average quarterly balance > ₹50,000

---

### 3. Professional Current Account

**Target Customers:** Doctors, lawyers, chartered accountants, architects, consultants

**Eligibility:** Valid professional registration certificate required

**Key Features:**
- Minimum balance requirement: ₹25,000
- Free cash deposits: Up to ₹2 lakh per month
- Free cheque book: 100 leaves per quarter
- Professional indemnity insurance: ₹5 lakh cover
- Tax advisory services (3 consultations per year)
- Overdraft facility: Up to ₹10 lakh (subject to approval)

**Transaction Limits:**
- Daily withdrawal: ₹2 lakh
- Monthly free transactions: 200

**Fee Structure:**
- Account opening: ₹2,000
- Annual maintenance: ₹10,000
- Minimum balance penalty: ₹1,000 per quarter

## Account Opening Requirements

### Documentation for Sole Proprietors
- PAN card (mandatory)
- Business registration certificate or GST registration
- Identity and address proof of proprietor
- Business address proof
- Recent passport-size photographs

### Documentation for Partnership Firms
- Partnership deed
- PAN card of firm
- Identity and address proof of all partners
- Board resolution for account opening
- Specimen signatures of authorized signatories

### Documentation for Private Limited Companies
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- PAN card of company
- Board resolution for account opening
- Identity proof of directors
- Authorized signatory list with specimen signatures

### Documentation for LLP
- LLP registration certificate
- LLP agreement
- PAN card of LLP
- Identity proof of designated partners
- Authorization for account operation

## Transaction Services

### Cash Management Services
- Cash deposit facility at branch counters
- Cash pickup service (charges apply for amounts > ₹5 lakh)
- Cash delivery service available (prior notice required)
- Coin exchange facility

**Cash Deposit Charges (beyond free limit):**
- 0.1% of deposited amount (minimum ₹100)
- Applicable for deposits exceeding monthly free limit

### Cheque Services
- Cheque book issued within 3 working days
- Positive Pay system for cheques above ₹50,000 (recommended)
- Cheque truncation system (CTS) compliant
- Outstation cheque collection: ₹100 per cheque

### Digital Payment Services
- NEFT/RTGS facility (charges as per RBI guidelines)
- Bulk payment processing
- Standing instructions facility
- Virtual payment address (UPI for business)

---

## Overdraft Facility

### Eligibility
- Account operational for minimum 6 months
- Satisfactory transaction history
- No cheque bounces in last 6 months
- Credit score evaluation required

### Overdraft Limits
- Basic Current Account: Up to ₹5 lakh
- Premium Current Account: Up to ₹50 lakh
- Professional Current Account: Up to ₹10 lakh

### Interest Charges
- Interest rate: 12-14% per annum (based on credit profile)
- Interest calculated on daily outstanding balance
- Renewal required annually

### Collateral Requirements
- Unsecured overdraft: Up to ₹5 lakh (subject to credit assessment)
- Secured overdraft: Against fixed deposits, property, or securities

---

## Account Maintenance

### Statement Services
- Monthly statements sent via email (free)
- Physical statement: ₹100 per quarter
- Online statement download available 24/7

### Account Monitoring
- Regular review of account activity
- Alert for unusual transactions
- Dormancy warning if no transactions for 6 months

### Closure Procedure
- Written request from authorized signatory required
- All pending cheques must be cleared
- Outstanding overdraft must be settled
- Final statement provided within 7 working days
- Closure charges: ₹2,000 if closed within 1 year

---

## Compliance and Regulatory

### KYC Requirements
- Initial KYC at account opening
- Periodic KYC update every 2 years for high-risk customers
- Annual review of business activity

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
- Cash transactions above ₹10 lakh reported to authorities
- Suspicious transaction monitoring
- Source of funds verification for large deposits

### GST Compliance
- GST charged on all applicable services
- GST invoices generated for service charges
- GST registration number to be provided by customer
