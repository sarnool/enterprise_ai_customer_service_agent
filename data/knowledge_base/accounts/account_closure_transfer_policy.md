## Account Closure

### Voluntary Closure by Customer

**Eligibility:**
- Account holder can close account at any time
- No lock-in period (except term deposits)
- All account holders must consent for joint accounts

**Process:**
1. Submit account closure request form at any branch
2. Return unused cheque leaves and debit card
3. Clear all pending transactions and dues
4. Settle any outstanding loans/overdrafts linked to account
5. Receive final balance via cheque or transfer

**Timeline:** 5-7 working days from request submission

**Closure Charges:**
- ₹500 if closed within 1 year of opening
- ₹200 if closed within 1-2 years
- No charge if closed after 2 years
- Waived for deceased account holders

---

### Bank-Initiated Closure

**Grounds for Closure:**
- Account dormant for more than 3 years
- Fraudulent activity detected
- Non-compliance with KYC norms
- Repeated cheque bounces (more than 3 in 6 months)
- Account used for money laundering

**Notice Period:** 30 days notice provided to customer

## Fund Transfer Between Own Accounts

### Internal Transfer (Same Bank)

**Methods:**
- Internet banking (instant)
- Mobile banking (instant)
- Branch request (same day)
- Standing instructions

**Charges:** Free for transfers between own accounts

**Limits:**
- No limit for internal transfers
- Instant processing 24/7

---

### External Transfer (Another Bank)

**NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer):**
- Processed in batches (hourly during banking hours)
- Available 24/7 including holidays
- Charges: ₹5 per transaction (waived for premium accounts)

**RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement):**
- Instant transfer for amounts ≥ ₹2 lakh
- Available 7 AM to 6 PM on working days
- Charges: ₹30 + GST

**IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):**
- Instant 24/7 transfer
- Limit: Up to ₹5 lakh per day
- Charges: ₹10 per transaction

---

## Account Transfer (Change of Location)

**Branch Transfer:**
- Customer can transfer account to any branch
- No charges for branch transfer
- Process completed in 7 working days
- Cheque book and debit card remain valid

**Required Documents:**
- Account transfer request form
- Address proof of new location
- Existing account details

---

## Balance Refund Methods

**Options:**
1. **Cheque:** Issued within 5 working days
2. **Transfer to Another Account:** Provide beneficiary details
3. **Direct Cash:** For balance < ₹50,000 (at branch counter)
4. **Demand Draft:** Charges apply (₹50 + GST)

## Special Closure Scenarios

### Deceased Account Holder

**Process:**
- Legal heir or nominee submits death certificate
- Claim form with required documents
- Bank freezes account upon notification
- Settlement within 15 days for nominee accounts
- May take longer for succession certificate cases

**Required Documents:**
- Death certificate
- Claim form
- Legal heir certificate or succession certificate
- Identity proof of claimant

---

### Dormant Account Reactivation

**Reactivation Process:**
- Submit written request at branch
- Update KYC documents
- Small transaction (deposit/withdrawal) to activate
- No reactivation charges

**Dormancy Definition:**
- No customer-initiated transaction for 2 years
- Automatic classification by system

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I close my account online?**  
A: No, account closure requires branch visit for verification.

**Q: What happens to my fixed deposits?**  
A: Fixed deposits must be closed separately or transferred.

**Q: Will I lose my pending interest?**  
A: No, all accrued interest is paid at closure.
