## Overview

This policy outlines the features, eligibility criteria, interest rates, and operational guidelines for all savings account products offered by the bank.

---

## Savings Account Variants

### 1. Basic Savings Account

**Target Customers:** First-time account holders, students, low-income customers

**Key Features:**
- Minimum balance requirement: ₹500
- Free ATM transactions: 5 per month at any ATM
- Cheque book: 25 leaves per quarter (free)
- Mobile banking and internet banking enabled
- Debit card: Classic variant (free)

**Interest Rate:** 3.0% per annum (calculated daily, credited quarterly)

**Fee Structure:**
- Account opening: ₹0
- Annual maintenance: ₹200 (waived if average quarterly balance > ₹5,000)
- Minimum balance penalty: ₹100 per quarter if balance falls below ₹500

---

### 2. Premium Savings Account

**Target Customers:** Salaried professionals, business executives

**Key Features:**
- Minimum balance requirement: ₹25,000
- Unlimited free ATM transactions nationwide
- Cheque book: 100 leaves per quarter (free)
- Personalized debit card (Gold variant)
- Priority customer service
- Free demand drafts: 10 per quarter
- Complimentary insurance cover: ₹2 lakh personal accident insurance

**Interest Rate:** 3.5% per annum (calculated daily, credited quarterly)

**Fee Structure:**
- Account opening: ₹500
- Annual maintenance: ₹1,000 (waived if average quarterly balance > ₹50,000)
- Minimum balance penalty: ₹500 per quarter

---

### 3. Senior Citizen Savings Account

**Target Customers:** Individuals aged 60 years and above

**Key Features:**
- Minimum balance requirement: ₹10,000
- Free ATM transactions: 10 per month
- Special interest rate for senior citizens
- Free cheque book: 50 leaves per quarter
- Dedicated senior citizen helpline
- Doorstep banking service available

**Interest Rate:** 4.0% per annum (0.5% higher than regular savings)

**Fee Structure:**
- Account opening: ₹0
- Annual maintenance: ₹0 (completely waived for senior citizens)
- No minimum balance penalty for customers above 70 years

---

### 4. Student Savings Account

**Target Customers:** Students aged 10-25 years

**Eligibility:** Valid student ID card or school/college enrollment certificate required

**Key Features:**
- Zero minimum balance requirement
- Free ATM transactions: 8 per month
- Free debit card
- No annual maintenance charges until age 25
- Educational loan benefits: Pre-approved personal loans for higher education

**Interest Rate:** 3.0% per annum

**Fee Structure:**
- All fees waived for students
- Account automatically converts to Basic Savings Account at age 26

---

### 5. Salary Account

**Target Customers:** Salaried employees of corporate tie-up organizations

**Eligibility:** Employer must have corporate banking relationship with the bank

**Key Features:**
- Zero minimum balance requirement (as long as salary credit continues)
- Unlimited free ATM transactions
- Free cheque book: 100 leaves per quarter
- Overdraft facility: Up to 2x monthly salary (subject to credit approval)
- Priority processing for loans and credit cards

**Interest Rate:** 3.25% per annum

**Fee Structure:**
- All fees waived while salary credit is active
- Converts to Basic Savings Account if no salary credit for 3 consecutive months

## General Terms and Conditions

### Account Opening Requirements
- Identity proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID)
- Address proof (Utility bill, Aadhaar, Passport, Rental agreement)
- Recent passport-size photographs (2 copies)
- Minimum initial deposit as per account variant
- PAN card mandatory for accounts with opening balance > ₹50,000

### Interest Calculation
- Interest calculated on daily balance
- Credited to account quarterly (March 31, June 30, September 30, December 31)
- TDS deducted if interest income exceeds ₹40,000 per annum (₹50,000 for senior citizens)

### Transaction Limits
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000
- Daily POS transaction limit: ₹2,00,000
- Online transfer limit: ₹5,00,000 per day (can be increased on request)

### Account Closure
- Request can be submitted at any branch
- Closure charges: ₹500 if account closed within 1 year of opening
- No charges if account closed after 1 year
- Balance refunded via cheque or transferred to another account

---

## Fee Waivers and Concessions

**Automatic Waivers:**
- Senior citizens: All maintenance fees waived
- Students: All fees waived until age 26
- Salary accounts: Fees waived during active salary credit

**Conditional Waivers:**
- Minimum balance penalty waived if average quarterly balance maintained
- Annual maintenance waived for Premium accounts with ₹50,000 average balance

**Special Circumstances:**
- Medical emergencies: Minimum balance penalties may be waived (discretionary, branch manager approval)
- Natural disasters: All penalties waived for affected customers (3-month grace period)

---

## Compliance and Regulatory Notes

- Accounts comply with RBI guidelines on savings bank accounts
- KYC norms must be completed within 30 days of account opening
- Dormant account policy: Accounts with no transactions for 2 years classified as dormant
- Anti-money laundering guidelines apply to all accounts
