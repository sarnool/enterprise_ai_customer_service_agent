## Savings Account Fee Structure

### Minimum Balance Requirements

| Account Type | Metro/Urban | Semi-Urban | Rural |
|-------------|-------------|------------|-------|
| Basic Savings | ₹1,000 | ₹500 | ₹500 |
| Premium Savings | ₹25,000 | ₹15,000 | ₹10,000 |
| Senior Citizen | ₹10,000 | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| Student | ₹0 | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| Salary Account | ₹0 | ₹0 | ₹0 |

**Note:** Balances calculated as Average Monthly Balance (AMB) or Average Quarterly Balance (AQB)

---

### Penalty for Non-Maintenance

**Basic Savings Account:**
- Falls below ₹500: ₹100 per quarter
- Falls below ₹250: ₹150 per quarter

**Premium Savings Account:**
- Falls below ₹25,000: ₹500 per quarter
- Falls below ₹10,000: ₹750 per quarter

**Senior Citizen:**
- No penalty for customers above 70 years
- ₹200 per quarter for 60-70 age group

---

## Fee Waiver Conditions

### Automatic Waivers
- **Senior Citizens (70+):** All fees waived
- **Students:** Fees waived until age 26
- **Salary Accounts:** Waived during active salary credit
- **PWD (Persons with Disabilities):** 50% discount on all fees

### Conditional Waivers
- **High Balance Customers:** Waived if AQB > 2x minimum requirement
- **Digital-only Customers:** 25% discount for those using only digital channels
- **Long-term Relationship:** Fees waived for customers with 10+ years relationship

### Discretionary Waivers
Available in exceptional circumstances:
- Medical emergencies (hospitalization > 15 days)
- Natural disasters or calamities
- Job loss or business closure
- Death of earning family member

**Process:** Submit request with supporting documents at branch. Branch manager approval  required.

## Transaction Charges

### ATM Transactions
- **Own Bank ATMs:** Free unlimited
- **Other Bank ATMs:** 
  - Metro: 5 free per month, then ₹20 per transaction
  - Non-metro: 5 free per month, then ₹10 per transaction

### Cheque Services
- **Cheque book issuance:** ₹2 per leaf (beyond free quota)
- **Cheque return (insufficient funds):** ₹500 per instance
- **Stop payment:** ₹100 per cheque
- **Duplicate cheque book:** ₹100

### Fund Transfer
- **NEFT:** ₹5 per transaction (free for premium accounts)
- **RTGS:** ₹30 for transactions > ₹2 lakh
- **IMPS:** ₹10 per transaction

### Statement Charges
- **Physical statement:** ₹100 per quarter
- **E-statement:** Free
- **Duplicate statement:** ₹50 per quarter

## Debit Card Charges

### Issuance and Annual Fee
- **Classic Card:** ₹0 (free)
- **Gold Card:** ₹500 per year
- **Platinum Card:** ₹1,500 per year

### Replacement
- **Lost/Stolen Card:** ₹100
- **Damaged Card:** ₹50
- **Renewal:** Free (auto-renewed before expiry)

## Other Fees

### Account Services
- **Account closure (within 1 year):** ₹500
- **Duplicate passbook:** ₹100
- **Balance certificate:** ₹100
- **Interest certificate:** ₹100

### Loan Services (linked to account)
- **Loan processing fee:** 0.5-2% of loan amount
- **Prepayment charges:** 2-4% of outstanding amount
- **Cheque bounce (EMI):** ₹750 per instance

- GST component shown separately in statements
