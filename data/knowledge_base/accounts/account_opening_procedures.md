## Overview

This document provides step-by-step procedures for opening savings and current accounts, including documentation requirements, verification processes, KYC compliance, and account activation timelines.

---

## General Account Opening Process

### Step 1: Customer Walk-in or Online Application

**Branch Process:**
1. Customer visits branch with required documents
2. Account opening form issued (Form 60 or Form 61 as applicable)
3. Customer fills form with assistance from bank staff
4. Initial screening for account eligibility

**Online Process:**
1. Customer initiates application via internet banking portal or mobile app
2. Video KYC or Aadhaar-based e-KYC initiated
3. Digital submission of documents
4. Application forwarded to nearest branch for verification

---

### Step 2: Document Submission

**Mandatory Documents for all Accounts:**

1. **Identity Proof (any one):**
   - Aadhaar Card (preferred)
   - PAN Card
   - Passport
   - Voter ID Card
   - Driving License

2. **Address Proof (any one):**
   - Aadhaar Card
   - Passport
   - Utility bill (electricity, water, gas) not older than 3 months
   - Bank account statement
   - Registered rent agreement

3. **Photographs:**
   - 2 recent passport-size color photographs
   - Photograph specification: 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm, white background

4. **PAN Card:**
   - Mandatory for all accounts (except Basic Savings Account with balance < ₹50,000)
   - Form 60 (declaration of non-availability of PAN) accepted in exceptional cases

**Additional Documents for Current Accounts:**
- Business registration documents
- GST registration certificate
- Partnership deed / Incorporation certificate (as applicable)
- Board resolution for corporate accounts

**Additional Documents for Minor Accounts:**
- Birth certificate of minor
- Identity and address proof of guardian
- Guardian's photograph and signature

---

### Step 3: KYC Verification

**In-Person Verification (IPV):**
- Bank official verifies customer's identity by physical presence
- Photograph captured at branch
- Biometric authentication (Aadhaar-linked)
- Original documents verified against submitted copies

**Video KYC (V-KYC):**
- Available for savings account opening only
- Live video call with bank's authorized representative
- Real-time document verification
- Geo-tagging and time-stamping of verification session
- Completed within 10 minutes

**Aadhaar-based e-KYC:**
- OTP sent to Aadhaar-registered mobile number
- Customer authorizes use of Aadhaar details
- Instant data retrieval from UIDAI
- No physical documents required

---

### Step 4: Initial Deposit

**Deposit Methods:**
- Cash deposit at branch counter
- Cheque deposit (from existing bank account)
- Online transfer from another bank (NEFT/RTGS)
- Demand draft

**Minimum Initial Deposit:**
- Basic Savings Account: ₹500
- Premium Savings Account: ₹25,000
- Senior Citizen Savings Account: ₹10,000
- Student Savings Account: ₹100
- Salary Account: ₹0 (can be nil)
- Basic Current Account: ₹10,000
- Premium Current Account: ₹1,00,000

---

### Step 5: Account Activation

**Processing Timeline:**
- Branch opening: 24-48 hours (1-2 working days)
- Online opening with V-KYC: 24 hours
- Aadhaar e-KYC: Instant to 4 hours

**Account Number Generation:**
- System-generated 14-digit account number
- Account number assigned upon successful KYC verification
- SMS and email notification sent to customer

**Welcome Kit Dispatch:**
Contains:
- Personalized cheque book (if applicable)
- Debit card (dispatched within 5-7 working days)
- Account statement
- Internet banking user ID and temporary password
- Mobile banking activation instructions

## Special Account Opening Scenarios

### Joint Accounts

**Types of Joint Accounts:**
- **Former or Survivor:** Either account holder can operate independently
- **Latter or Survivor:** Only on death of one holder, survivor can operate
- **Anyone or Survivor:** Any one holder can operate
- **Joint:** All holders must sign jointly for transactions

**Documentation:**
- All joint holders must be present for account opening (or provide power of attorney)
- Identity and address proof of all holders required
- Nomination facility recommended

---

### Minor Accounts

**Eligibility:**
- Account opened in name of minor (below 18 years)
- Natural guardian or court-appointed guardian operates account

**Conversion to Major Account:**
- Automatic conversion when minor attains 18 years
- Minor must visit branch with age proof
- Fresh KYC and signature specimen required
- New debit card and cheque book issued

---

### NRI Accounts

**Account Types:**
- NRE (Non-Resident External) Account
- NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) Account

**Additional Documentation:**
- Valid passport with visa
- Overseas address proof
- Proof of NRI status (employment letter, residence permit)

**Special Procedures:**
- Accounts must comply with FEMA regulations
- Repatriation restrictions apply to NRO accounts
- Power of Attorney can be registered for account operation

## Account Opening for Specific Customer Segments

### Senior Citizens (60+ years)

**Fast-track Processing:**
- Designated senior citizen desk at branches
- Assisted account opening
- Home visit service available (on request)

**Relaxations:**
- Minimum balance penalties waived for customers above 70 years
- Enhanced interest rates (additional 0.5% on savings)
- Free doorstep banking

---

### Students

**Simplified Process:**
- Student ID or school/college enrollment certificate required
- Zero minimum balance
- Parent/guardian may be joint holder (optional)
- Financial literacy kit provided

**Automatic Conversion:**
- Account converts to Basic Savings Account at age 26
- Customer notified 3 months in advance
- No action required from customer

---

### Salary Account Holders

**Corporate Tie-up Requirement:**
- Employer must have banking relationship with the bank
- Bulk account opening process

**Documentation:**
- Simplified documentation with employer verification
- Salary certificate from HR department
- Standard identity and address proofs

**Privileges:**
- Zero balance account
- Pre-approved overdraft facility (subject to credit check)
- Priority loan processing

## Compliance and Regulatory Requirements

### Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Guidelines

**Know Your Customer (KYC) Norms:**
- Mandatory for all accounts
- Risk categorization: Low, Medium, High risk
- Periodic KYC update required

**Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA):**
- Customer Due Diligence (CDD) mandatory
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk customers
- Reporting of suspicious transactions

### Aadhaar Linking

**Mandatory Linking:**
- All existing account holders must link Aadhaar
- Deadline as per government notification
- Accounts may be frozen if not linked within stipulated time

**Exemptions:**
- NRI accounts
- Foreign nationals
- Minors without Aadhaar

---

## Account Opening Rejection Criteria

**Grounds for Rejection:**
- Incomplete or invalid documentation
- Negative database check (defaulter list, CIBIL)
- Suspicious source of funds
- Account opening purpose not aligned with customer profile
- Customer on sanctions list or adverse media
- Failure in KYC verification

**Communication:**
- Rejection communicated to customer within 7 working days
- Reason for rejection provided (unless legally restricted)
- Documents returned to customer

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Can I open an account without visiting the branch?**  
A: Yes, through video KYC or Aadhaar e-KYC. Account opened digitally within 24 hours.

**Q2: How long does it take to receive my debit card?**  
A: Debit card dispatched within 5-7 working days via courier to registered address.

**Q3: Can I open multiple savings accounts?**  
A: Yes, but only one account can be linked to government subsidy schemes. Multiple accounts allowed for different purposes.

**Q4: What if I don't have a PAN card?**  
A: For savings account with balance < ₹50,000, Form 60 can be submitted. PAN is mandatory for higher balances and all current accounts.

**Q5: Can I nominate someone for my account?**  
A: Yes, nomination facility available at account opening or anytime later. Nomination helps in smooth transfer of funds in case of account holder's death.
