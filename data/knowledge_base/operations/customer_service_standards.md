## Customer Interaction Standards

### Branch Banking

**Reception:**
- Greet customer within 30 seconds of entry
- Token issued immediately
- Estimated wait time communicated

**Waiting Time:**
- Maximum waiting time: 15 minutes (during normal hours)
- Express counters for senior citizens and persons with disabilities
- Priority service for premium customers

**Service Counter:**
- Staff name visible on desk
- Polite greeting
- Active listening to customer query
- Clear explanation of process/product
- Timely resolution

**Transaction Completion:**
- Acknowledge receipt with thank you
- Provide transaction slip/receipt
- Inform about alternative channels (mobile/internet banking)

---

### Phone Banking

**Call Answered:** Within 60 seconds (or 3 rings)

**Greeting:** Professional and warm

**Verification:** Customer ID or last 4 digits of account/card

**Issue Resolution:**
- Resolved on first call (80% target)
- If escalation needed: Ticket number issued
- Callback commitment honored

**Call Closure:** Ensure customer satisfaction before ending call

**Quality:** Calls monitored for quality assurance

---

### Digital Banking (Mobile/Internet)

**App/Website Availability:** 99.5% uptime

**Login Response Time:** < 3 seconds

**Transaction Processing:** Real-time

**Support:**
- In-app chat support during 9 AM - 9 PM
- Email response: Within 24 hours
- Chatbot available 24/7

---

### Email Communication

**Acknowledgement:** Within 4 working hours

**Resolution:** Within 48-72 hours

**Escalation:** If not resolved in 72 hours, escalated to senior team

**Communication:** Professional, clear, and courteous

### Resolution Timeline

| Complaint Type | Resolution Time |
|----------------|----------------|
| Account statement discrepancy | 7 days |
| Failed transaction | 3-5 days |

### Escalation Matrix

**Level 1:** Branch Manager (7 days)

**Level 2:** Regional Manager (15 days)

**Level 3:** Principal Nodal Officer (30 days)

**Level 4:** Banking Ombudsman (RBI) (if unresolved after 30 days)

**Contact Details:**
- Nodal Officer: nodalofficer@bank.com
- Banking Ombudsman: Available on RBI website

**Transaction Services:**
- NEFT/RTGS processed same day

- Loan sanction communicated within stated timelines

- Prepayment allowed without penalty (floating rate loans)

**Card Services:**
- Card blocked immediately upon request
- Replacement card within 5 days

---

### Compensation for Service Failure

**Scenarios:**
- **Delayed NEFT/RTGS:** Compensation as per RBI guidelines (₹100 per day)
- **Failed ATM transaction with debit:** Reversal within 5 days + ₹100 per day compensation
- **Unauthorized transaction (if bank's fault):** Full refund + interest
- **Delayed loan disbursement (non-customer fault):** Waiver of processing fee or interest compensation

## Customer Rights

### Right to Fair Treatment
- Non-discriminatory service
- Privacy and confidentiality
- Suitability of products recommended

### Right to Information
- Clear, accurate, and timely information
- Disclosure of fees, charges, and terms
- Access to account statements

### Right to Grievance Redressal
- Register complaints through multiple channels
- Timely resolution
- Escalation to Banking Ombudsman if not satisfied

### Right to Protection
- Security of deposits (upto ₹5 lakh per account under DICGC)
- Data privacy
- Protection against mis-selling

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How can I check the status of my complaint?**
A: Use complaint reference number to track via mobile app, internet banking, or call customer care.

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with the resolution?**
A: Escalate to next level. If unresolved after 30 days, approach Banking Ombudsman.

**Q: Are there charges for  customer service?**
A: No. Customer care calls, complaint registration, and branch services are free.

**Q: How do I suggest improvements?**
A: Email suggestions@bank.com or submit via mobile app/internet banking.
