## Overview

This document provides procedures for lodging, tracking, resolving, and escalating customer complaints. It ensures transparency, accountability, and timely resolution of grievances.

---

## What Constitutes a Complaint?

**Definition:** Any expression of dissatisfaction with bank's products, services, staff behavior, processes, or policies.

**Common Complaint Types:**
- Deficiency in service
- Unauthorized transactions
- Hidden charges or incorrect billing
- Delayed transaction processing
- Poor staff behavior
- Account-related issues
- Card disputes
- Loan servicing problems
- Digital banking issues

---

## Complaint Registration Channels

### 1. Branch
- Visit nearest branch
- Fill complaint form or submit written complaint
- Submit to Branch Manager or Customer Service Desk
- Acknowledgement slip with reference number issued

**Advantages:** Face-to-face interaction, immediate acknowledgement

---

### 2. Customer Care Helpline
- Call toll-free: **1800-XXX-CARE** (24/7)
- Complaint registered by executive
- Reference number provided via SMS and email

**Advantages:** Convenient, available 24/7

---

### 3. Email
- Send detailed complaint to: **complaints@bank.com**
- Include: Name, account/card number, contact details, nature of complaint
- Auto-acknowledgement with reference number within 4 hours

**Advantages:** Documented trail, can attach supporting documents

---

### 4. Internet Banking
- Login → Customer Service → Register Complaint
- Select complaint category
- Provide details
- Submit with attachments (if applicable)
- Reference number displayed immediately

**Advantages:** Real-time tracking, document upload facility

---

### 5. Mobile Banking App
- Open app → Help & Support → Complaints
- Select issue category
- Describe problem
- Upload screenshots if needed
- Receive reference number instantly

**Advantages:** On-the-go access, screenshot upload

---

### 6. Written Letter
- Address to: Branch Manager or Nodal Officer
- Include all relevant details and supporting documents
- Send via registered post
- Acknowledgement sent within 7 days

**Advantages:** Formal record, suitable for complex issues

---

## Complaint Registration Process

### Step 1: Acknowledgement
- Complaint reference number (CRN) generated
- Format: CMP/YYYY/MM/XXXXX
- SMS and email confirmation sent within 4 hours
- Contains: CRN, expected resolution date, contact details

---

### Step 2: Complaint Assignment
- Complaint routed to appropriate department
- Assigned to resolution officer
- Customer notified of officer details

---

### Step 3: Investigation
- Resolution officer reviews complaint
- Verifies facts and supporting documents
- May contact customer for additional information
- Liaises with relevant departments

---

### Step 4: Resolution
- Solution identified
- Customer informed
- Action taken (refund, reversal, correction, etc.)
- Confirmation sent via SMS/email

---

### Step 5: Closure
- Complaint marked resolved
- Customer feedback requested
- Closure confirmation sent

**Standard Timeline:** 7-15 days (varies by complaint type)

## Escalation Matrix

If complaint not resolved within stipulated time, automatic escalation occurs.

### Level 1: Branch Manager / Department Head
**Timeline:** 0-7 days
**Scope:** Initial complaint handling

**Contact:** Via branch or departmental email

---

### Level 2: Regional Manager
**Timeline:** 8-15 days (if Level 1 unresolved)
**Scope:** Review and override Level 1 decision if justified

**Contact:** Through Level 1 or direct email to regional office

---

### Level 3: Principal Nodal Officer
**Timeline:** 16-30 days (if Level 2 unresolved)
**Scope:** Final internal review

**Contact:**
- Email: nodalofficer@bank.com
- Phone: 1800-XXX-NODE
- Address: [Head Office Address]

**Responsibilities:**
- Independent review of complaint
- Binds Level 1 and 2 to implement decision
- Interface with Banking Ombudsman

---

### Level 4: Banking Ombudsman (RBI)
**Timeline:** After 30 days or if reply not received
**Scope:** External dispute resolution mechanism

**When to Approach:**
- Complaint unresolved after 30 days
- Bank's final reply not acceptable
- No response from bank within 30 days

**How to Approach:**
1. File complaint with Banking Ombudsman (online or written)
2. Attach bank's complaint reference number and correspondence
3. No fee for filing

**Banking Ombudsman Contact:**
- Website: https://cms.rbi.org.in
- Jurisdiction: Based on bank branch location
- Find nearest Ombudsman on RBI website

**Ombudsman Decision:**
- Binding if award is up to ₹10 lakh and accepted by customer
- Bank must comply within specified time

## Complaint Categories and Handling

### 1. Transaction Disputes

**Examples:**
- Amount debited but merchant says transaction failed
- Duplicate charge
- Wrong amount debited

**Resolution Process:**
1. Transaction details verified
2. Merchant contacted (for card transactions)
3. Chargeback initiated if merchant confirms non-delivery/cancellation
4. Provisional credit issued (for claims ≤ ₹25,000)
5. Final decision within 30 days

---

### 2. Unauthorized Transactions

**Examples:**
- Card misuse
- Online fraud
- Account hacking

**Immediate Action:**
1. Block card/account immediately
2. File FIR (if amount is substantial)
3. Investigation initiated

**Liability:**
- Zero liability if reported within 24 hours
- Limited liability (max ₹10,000) if reported within 3 days
- Full liability if reported after 7 days

**Timeline:** Investigation completed within 15 days

---

### 3. Service Deficiency

**Examples:**
- Delayed service
- Incorrect information provided
- Staff misbehavior

**Resolution Process:**
1. Incident verified through CCTV, call recordings, or staff statement
2. Corrective action taken
3. Staff counseling or disciplinary action if needed
4. Customer provided feedback on corrective measures

**Timeline:** 7 working days

---

### 4. Loan-Related Complaints

**Examples:**
- Delayed disbursement
- Incorrect EMI calculation
- Prepayment issues
- Hidden charges

**Resolution Process:**
1. Loan account reviewed
2. Calculations verified
3. If error found: Correction made, refund issued
4. If no error: Detailed explanation provided

**Timeline:** 10-15 days

---

### 5. Digital Banking Issues

**Examples:**
- App/website not working
- Transaction failure
- Login issues

**Resolution Process:**
1. Technical team investigates
2. Issue resolved or workaround provided
3. Compensation for failed transactions (if applicable)

**Timeline:** 3-5 days

## Customer Rights During Complaint Process

### Right to Information
- Know status of complaint at any time
- Track via mobile app, internet banking, or customer care
- Receive regular updates (every 7 days for pending complaints)

### Right to Fair Treatment
- Complaint handled objectively
- No bias or discrimination
- Privacy maintained

### Right to Escalate
- Escalate if dissatisfied with resolution
- Approach Banking Ombudsman if unresolved

### Right to Compensation
- As per bank's compensation policy
- For service failures (delayed NEFT/RTGS, ATM failures, etc.)
- Interest on delayed refunds

---

## Compensation for Service Failures

### Failed ATM Transaction (Amount Debited)
- Reversal within 5 days + ₹100 per day compensation after 5 days

### Delayed NEFT/RTGS
- ₹100 per day penalty paid to customer (as per RBI guidelines)

### Unauthorized Electronic Transaction (Bank's Fault)
- Full refund + interest @ savings account rate
- From date of debit to date of refund

### Delayed Loan Disbursement (Non-Customer Fault)
- Waiver of processing fee OR
- Interest rate reduction for initial period

### Card Dispute Resolution Delay
- If resolution delayed beyond 90 days: ₹100 compensation

## Complaint Closure

### Satisfactory Resolution
- Customer confirms acceptance
- Complaint marked "Resolved"
- Feedback form sent to customer

### Unsatisfactory Resolution
- Customer informs within 7 days of resolution communication
- Complaint reopened or escalated to next level

### Customer Non-Response
- If customer doesn't respond within 15 days
- Complaint marked "Closed - No Response"
- Can be reopened within 30 days on customer request

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does complaint resolution take?**
A: Typically 7-15 days. Complex cases may take up to 30 days. You'll be kept informed.

**Q: Can I track my complaint?**
A: Yes, use your complaint reference number to track via mobile app, internet banking, or call customer care.

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with the resolution?**
A: You can escalate to the next level. Ultimately, you have the right to approach the Banking Ombudsman.

**Q: Is there any charge for filing a complaint?**
A: No, complaint registration and resolution are completely free.

**Q: Can I file a complaint on behalf of someone else?**
A: Only if you have proper authorization (power of attorney or legal heir certificate).

**Q: What if the complaint is against a specific staff member?**
A: You can mention this in your complaint. Appropriate disciplinary action will be taken if the complaint is valid.

**Q: Will filing a complaint affect my relationship with the bank?**
A: Absolutely not. Complaints help us improve. Your banking services will continue normally.

**Q: Can I withdraw my complaint?**
A: Yes, at any time before closure. Inform via the same channel you used for registration.
