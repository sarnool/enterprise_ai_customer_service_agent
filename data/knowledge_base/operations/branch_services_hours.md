## Services Available at Branches

### Account Services

**Account Opening:**
- Savings accounts (all variants)
- Current accounts
- Fixed deposits
- Recurring deposits

**Account Maintenance:**
- KYC updates
- Nomination changes
- Address updates
- Name changes
- Account closure requests

**Passbook Services:**
- New passbook issuance
- Passbook printing
- Duplicate passbook

---

### Cash Services

**Deposits:**
- Cash deposit (to own account or third-party)
- Cheque deposit
- Demand draft deposit

**Withdrawals:**
- Cash withdrawal (up to ₹25,000 with passbook/token)
- Withdrawal slips for bearer cheques
- Third-party withdrawal (with proper authorization)

**Cash Deposit Limits:**
- Own account: No limit (large amounts require PAN)
- Third-party: Up to ₹50,000 per day (new limit as per income tax guidelines)

---

### Cheque and Draft Services

**Cheque Book:**
- Issue new cheque book (25/50/100 leaves)
- Collect spare cheque leaves

**Demand Drafts:**
- Purchase demand draft (₹1 minimum, no maximum)
- DD cancellation and reissuance
- Charges: ₹50 + GST (up to ₹5,000), ₹100 + GST (above ₹5,000)

**Pay Order / Banker's Cheque:**
- Issued within same city
- Charges: ₹25 + GST

---

### Locker Services

**Locker Rental:**
- Small, medium, large, extra-large sizes
- Annual rent: ₹1,000 - ₹10,000 (varies by size and location)

**Access:**
- During banking hours (10 AM - 3:30 PM)
- Dual key system (bank + customer)
- Visitor register maintained

**Waiting List:**
- Enroll for locker if not available
- Allotted on first-come, first-served basis

---

### Card Services

**Issuance:**
- Apply for new debit/credit card
- Collect replacement card (if delivered to branch)

**Services:**
- Card activation assistance
- PIN generation (debit card)
- Card blocking

**Charges:** As per account/card type

---

### Loan Services

**Application:**
- Personal loan applications
- Home loan applications
- Auto loan applications
- Gold loan (select branches)

**Servicing:**
- EMI payment via cheque
- Loan closure
- NOC collection
- Loan statement requests

**Relationship Manager:**
- Personalized loan consultations
- Home loan site visits arranged

---

### Foreign Exchange Services

**Currency Exchange:**
- Buy foreign currency (cash/forex card)
- Sell foreign currency
- Popular currencies available at select branches

**Travel Card:**
- Multi-currency forex card
- Instant issuance at select branches

**Advance Booking:**
- For large amounts or less-common currencies
- 48 hours' notice preferred

**Charges:**
- As per current exchange rates

## Customer Etiquette

**For Smooth Service:**
- Take token and wait for your turn
- Keep documents ready
- Fill forms in advance (available at branch or online)
- Respect staff and fellow customers

**Security Tips:**
- Don't share PIN/password with anyone (including bank staff)
- Shield keypad while entering PIN
- Verify receipt before leaving counter
- Report suspicious activity immediately
