## Overview

This document outlines key regulatory compliance requirements that govern banking operations in India. It covers RBI guidelines, anti-money laundering (AML) norms, data protection, consumer protection, and audit requirements.

---

## Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Compliance

### Know Your Customer (KYC) Norms

**Mandatory Requirements:**
- Customer Due Diligence (CDD) at account opening
- Periodic KYC updates (every 2-10 years based on risk)
- Aadhaar linking for existing accounts
- PAN mandatory for high-value transactions

**Risk Categorization:**
- Low, Medium, High risk classification
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk customers
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) identification

**Compliance Monitoring:**
- Internal KYC audit quarterly
- Non-compliant accounts: Partial freezing after due date
- Reporting to RBI on KYC status

---

### Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA)

**Customer Due Diligence (CDD):**
- Verify identity and address
- Understand nature of business/source of funds
- Ongoing monitoring of transactions

**Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR):**
- Transactions not consistent with customer profile
- Large cash deposits without clear source
- Sudden increase in account activity
- Structuring (breaking large amounts into smaller transactions to evade reporting)

**Reporting to FIU-IND:**
- Cash transactions above ₹10 lakh (CTR - Cash Transaction Report)
- Suspicious transactions (STR - within 7 days of detection)
- Cross-border wire transfers

**Record Retention:**
- KYC records: 5 years after account closure
- Transaction records: 5 years from transaction date

---

### Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA)

**NRI Accounts:**
- Separate account types: NRE, NRO
- Compliance with repatriation rules
- Reporting to RBI for high-value transactions

**Liberalized Remittance Scheme (LRS):**
- Individuals can remit up to USD 250,000 per year
- Declaration and documentation required
- Tax compliance certificate (if applicable)

**Export/Import Transactions:**
- FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) issuance
- Reporting of export proceeds
- Import payment documentation

---

### Interest Rate Guidelines

**Savings Accounts:**
- Banks free to determine rates
- Must be transparent and published
- Changes notified 30 days in advance

**Loans:**
- Linked to external benchmark (Repo rate) or internal benchmark
- Reset clause must be clearly mentioned
- No hidden charges

**Fixed Deposits:**
- Fixed for tenure
- Premature withdrawal penalty allowed
- Senior citizen additional interest (up to 0.5%)

---

### Fair Practices Code

**Loan Disbursement:**
- Transparent communication of terms
- All charges disclosed upfront
- Facts sheet provided before sanction
- Cooling-off period for borrower

**Loan Recovery:**
- No harassment or coercion
- Recovery agents must follow code of conduct
- Complaints against agents investigated

**Third-Party Products:**
- Clear disclosure that product is not bank's
- Commission model disclosed
- No mis-selling

## Data Protection and Privacy

### RBI Guidelines on Data Localization

**Data Storage:**
- All payment system data to be stored in India
- Offshore storage permitted for processing, but copy must be in India
- Data retention as per RBI master directions

**Data Sharing:**
- Customer consent required for sharing with third parties
- Opt-in for marketing communications
- Right to withdraw consent

---

### Data Security

**Customer Information:**
- End-to-end encryption for online transactions
- Secure storage with access controls
- Regular security audits

**Cyber Security:**
- Compliance with RBI Cyber Security Framework
- Incident response plan in place
- Customer notification within 24 hours of data breach

---

## Consumer Protection

### Banking Ombudsman Scheme

**Customer Rights:**
- Free dispute resolution mechanism
- RBI-appointed ombudsmen in each region
- Decisions binding if award ≤ ₹10 lakh

**Bank's Obligations:**
- Provide complaint resolution within 30 days
- Display Banking Ombudsman contact details at branches
- Comply with Ombudsman's orders

---

### Deposit Insurance

**DICGC Coverage:**
- Deposits insured up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank
- Covers savings, current, fixed, recurring deposits
- Premium paid by bank to DICGC

**Customer Awareness:**
- Information displayed at branches
- Included in account opening kit

---

### Unclaimed Deposits

**Classification:**
- Accounts with no customer-induced transaction for 10 years
- Classified as unclaimed deposits

**Process:**
- Transferred to Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF)
- Customer can claim anytime with interest
- Bank assists in claim process

---

## Anti-Fraud Measures

### Prohibited Activities

**Customers:**
- Opening accounts with false identity
- Using accounts for money laundering
- Check kiting (writing checks against unfunded accounts)
- Card fraud

**Bank:**
- Regular transaction monitoring
- Suspicious activity flagged and reported
- Accounts frozen if fraud detected

---

### Reporting to Credit Bureaus

**CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF:**
- Loan and credit card data shared monthly
- Payment history reported
- Defaults and settlements marked

**Customer Rights:**
- Free annual credit report
- Right to dispute inaccuracies
- Bank to correct errors within 30 days

---

## Audit and Inspection

### Internal Audit

**Frequency:** Quarterly or as per risk profile

**Scope:**
- Compliance with RBI guidelines
- KYC and AML adherence
- Transaction monitoring
- Customer complaint resolution
- IT security

---

### RBI Inspection

**Frequency:** Annual or triggered (based on complaints/issues)

**Focus Areas:**
- Capital adequacy
- Asset quality
- Compliance with regulatory norms
- Customer service standards

**Findings:**
- Non-compliance areas identified
- Corrective action plan submitted
- Follow-up inspections

---

### Statutory Audit

**External Auditors:**
- Appointed as per RBI/Companies Act norms
- Annual financial audit
- Report submitted to RBI and shareholders

---

## Reporting Requirements

### To RBI

**Regular Reports:**
- Monthly: Credit information (loans disbursed, recoveries)
- Quarterly: NPA (Non-Performing Assets) status
- Annual: Financials, capital adequacy
- Event-based: Frauds above threshold, cyber incidents

**Formats:** As prescribed by RBI

---

### To FIU-IND

**Cash Transaction Reports (CTR):**
- All cash transactions ≥ ₹10 lakh
- Submitted monthly

**Suspicious Transaction Reports (STR):**
- Filed within 7 days of suspicion
- Confidential, customer not informed

---

### To SEBI (If Applicable)

For bancassurance and mutual fund distribution:
- Mis-selling complaints
- Distribution commissions
- Customer grievances

---

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

### For Banks

**RBI Penalties:**
- Monetary penalties up to ₹1 crore per violation
- Business restrictions (e.g., ban on new customer acquisition)
- Cancellation of license (in extreme cases)

**Reputation Risk:**
- Negative media coverage
- Loss of customer trust

---

### For Customers

**Account Freezing:**
- KYC non-compliance: Partial freeze
- Fraud/suspicious activity: Complete freeze

**Legal Action:**
- Money laundering: FIR under PMLA
- Fraud: Criminal prosecution

## Customer Responsibilities

**Compliance with Regulations:**
- Provide accurate information during account opening
- Update KYC periodically
- Declare source of large funds if asked
- Comply with tax regulations

**Reporting Obligations:**
- Report lost/stolen cards immediately
- Inform bank of suspicious activity in account
- Keep credentials secure
