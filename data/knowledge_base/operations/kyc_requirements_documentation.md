## Overview

Know Your Customer (KYC) is a mandatory process for all banking customers as per Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines and Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA). This document outlines KYC requirements, acceptable documents, verification procedures, and periodic update norms.

---

## What is KYC?

**Purpose:**
- Verify customer identity
- Confirm residential address
- Assess risk profile
- Prevent money laundering and fraud
- Comply with regulatory requirements

**When KYC is Required:**
- Opening new account
- Applying for loans or credit cards
- Conducting transactions above specified limits
- Periodic updates (every 2-10 years depending on risk category)

---

## KYC Compliance Categories

### Risk-Based Classification

**Low Risk:**
- Salaried employees of government and reputed organizations
- Senior citizens
- Existing customers with satisfactory transaction history
- **KYC Update:** Every 10 years

**Medium Risk:**
- Self-employed professionals
- Small business owners
- **KYC Update:** Every 8 years

**High Risk:**
- Non-resident Indians (NRIs)
- High net-worth individuals
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
- Customers from high-risk countries
- **KYC Update:** Every 2 years

## Officially Valid Documents (OVD)

### For Identity Proof

**Government-Issued Photo Identity:**
1. **Aadhaar Card** (Preferred)
2. **Passport**
3. **Voter ID Card**
4. **Driving License**
5. **PAN Card** (with photograph)
6. **NREGA Job Card**
7. **Government Employee ID**

**Note:** At least one document must contain photograph

---

### For Address Proof

**Accepted Documents (Not older than 3 months for utility bills):**
1. **Aadhaar Card** (preferred - serves both identity and address)
2. **Passport**
3. **Voter ID Card**
4. **Driving License**
5. **Utility Bills** (electricity, water, gas, landline phone)
6. **Bank Account Statement** (from another bank)
7. **Post Office Passbook**
8. **Registered Rent/Lease Agreement**
9. **Property Tax Receipt**
10. **Employer Certificate** (for company-provided accommodation)

**Special Cases:**
- If current address differs from document address, submit both old and new proofs
- For rented accommodation: Rent agreement + landlord's NOC

---

### PAN Card (Mandatory for Most Transactions)

**When PAN is Mandatory:**
- Opening accounts with initial balance > ₹50,000
- All credit card applications
- All loan applications
- Cash deposits/withdrawals > ₹50,000 in a day
- Fixed deposits > ₹5 lakh
- Purchase of forex > USD 25,000 (or equivalent)

**Alternative (If PAN not available):**
- **Form 60/61:** Declaration for non-availability of PAN
- Valid only for transactions up to specified limits
- Not applicable for high-value transactions

## KYC Verification Methods

### 1. In-Person Verification (IPV)

**Process:**
- Customer visits branch physically
- Bank official verifies identity face-to-face
- Original documents checked
- Photocopies certified by bank official
- Photograph taken and uploaded
- Signature specimen recorded

**Applicable for:**
- All account openings at branch
- High-value transactions
- High-risk customers

---

### 2. Video-Based KYC (V-KYC)

**Process:**
- Live video call with bank's authorized officer
- Customer shows original documents to camera
- Real-time verification
- Audio-visual recording with geo-tagging and time-stamping
- Consent recorded
- Completed within 10-15 minutes

**Requirements:**
- Smartphone with camera and internet
- Good lighting
- Original documents

**Advantages:**
- No branch visit required
- Instant account opening
- Convenient and fast

**Limitations:**
- Available only for resident individuals
- Not for NRIs or business accounts
- Savings account opening only (not current accounts)

---

### 3. Aadhaar-Based e-KYC

**Process:**
- Customer provides Aadhaar number and consent
- OTP sent to Aadhaar-registered mobile
- Biometric/OTP authentication
- Data fetched from UIDAI servers
- Instant verification

**Advantages:**
- Paperless and instant
- No physical documents needed
- Highly secure

**Mandatory Requirements:**
- Valid Aadhaar number
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
- Customer consent

---

### 4. Offline Aadhaar Verification

**Process:**
- Customer downloads Offline Aadhaar XML file from UIDAI
- Shares file and password with bank
- Bank validates electronically
- No data shared with UIDAI during verification

**Use Case:** For customers concerned about online Aadhaar authentication

## Simplified KYC (For Small Accounts)

### Eligibility
- Account balance not exceeding ₹50,000
- Total credit in account not exceeding ₹1 lakh per year

### Relaxed Documentation
- Self-attested photograph
- Basic identity document (can be self-attested copy)
- Simplified address verification

### Limitations
- Cannot be converted to regular account automatically
- Upgrade requires full KYC

---

## KYC for Special Categories

### Minor Accounts

**Documents Required:**
- Birth certificate of minor
- Identity and address proof of guardian
- Photograph of both minor and guardian
- Guardian's PAN

**Conversion at Majority:**
- When minor turns 18, account must be converted
- Fresh KYC of now-major individual
- New signature specimen, photographs

---

### NRI Accounts

**Additional Documents:**
- Valid passport with visa
- Overseas address proof (utility bill, bank statement, residence permit)
- Proof of NRI status (work permit, employment letter)
- PAN (if available) or Form 60

**Special Requirements:**
- Embassy attestation for certain documents
- Passport copy with entry/exit stamps
- Power of Attorney (if account operated by someone in India)

---

### Senior Citizens

**Concessions:**
- Doorstep banking for KYC updates
- Assisted documentation
- Video KYC from home

---

### Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)

**Definition:** Individuals holding or have held prominent public positions:
- Ministers, legislators
- Senior government officials
- Heads of public sector organizations
- Judicial officers
- Military generals

**Enhanced Due Diligence:**
- Source of wealth verification
- Approval from senior management
- Enhanced ongoing monitoring
- Family members also classified as PEPs

## Periodic KYC Update

### Update Frequency

| Risk Category | Update Frequency |
|---------------|------------------|
| Low Risk | Every 10 years |
| Medium Risk | Every 8 years |
| High Risk | Every 2 years |

**Reminder:**
- Bank sends reminder 3 months before due date
- Via SMS, email, letter

**Non-Compliance:**
- Account partially frozen (withdrawals limited)
- No new transactions allowed
- Account may be closed if KYC not updated within stipulated time

---

### Update Process

**Methods:**
1. **Visit Branch:** Submit updated documents
2. **Video KYC:** Schedule video call
3. **Online Upload:** Upload documents via internet banking (for existing customers)
4. **Aadhaar e-KYC:** One-click update if Aadhaar details are current

**Documents to Update:**
- If address changed: New address proof
- If identity document renewed: New copy (e.g., new passport)
- Recent photograph (if not done in last 10 years)

## Central KYC Registry (CKYCR)

### What is CKYCR?
- Centralized repository of KYC records
- Managed by Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest (CERSAI)
- One KYC for all financial institutions

### Benefits
- No repeat KYC across banks
- Faster account opening at other banks
- 14-digit KYC Identification Number (KIN) issued

### How It Works
1. First bank uploads KYC to CKYCR
2. KIN generated
3. Customer provides KIN to new bank
4. New bank downloads KYC from CKYCR
5. Instant verification

**Note:** Customer must inform new bank about existing KIN

## Special Situations

### Change of Address

**Process:**
1. Submit new address proof
2. Bank verifies (may send verification letter or call)
3. Address updated within 7 days
4. Confirmation SMS sent

**Documents Accepted:**
- Recent utility bill (not older than 3 months)
- Rent/lease agreement
- Employer certificate

---

### Change of Name

**Process:**
1. Submit name change proof:
   - Marriage certificate (for name change after marriage)
   - Gazette notification
   - Court order
2. Update all documents (PAN, Aadhaar, passport)
3. Bank updates records

**Impact:**
- Cheque book reissued
- Debit/credit card reissued with new name
- Nomination and signatures updated

---

### Duplicate Documents (Lost/Damaged)

**Process:**
- File FIR for lost documents (if applicable)
- Apply for duplicate from issuing authority
- Provide alternate proof until duplicate received
- Bank may mark account for enhanced monitoring

---

## Non-Compliance Consequences

### For Customers

**Partial Freeze:**
- After due date, account restricted
- Only withdrawals up to ₹10,000 per month allowed
- No credits allowed (except government benefits)
- No loans, cards, or additional services

**Account Closure:**
- If KYC not updated within 6 months of reminder
- Final notice issued
- Balance refunded via cheque

### For Bank

- Regulatory penalties
- Audit observations
- Cannot onboard new customers without compliant KYC

---

## Data Privacy and Security

### Customer Rights
- Right to know how data is used
- Right to access KYC records
- Right to correct errors in KYC data
- Right to withdraw consent (subject to regulatory compliance)

### Bank's Obligations
- Secure storage of KYC documents
- Encryption of digital records
- Access control (only authorized personnel)
- No sharing with third parties (except regulators)
- Destroy documents securely after retention period

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Aadhaar mandatory for bank accounts?**
A: Aadhaar is not 100% mandatory but highly recommended. Alternative documents accepted, but Aadhaar enables faster services.

**Q: Can I use the same documents for identity and address proof?**
A: Yes, single Officially Valid Document (like Aadhaar or Passport) can serve both purposes.

**Q: What if my address proof is in spouse's name?**
A: Submit spouse's document along with marriage certificate and declaration.

**Q: How long does KYC verification take?**
A: Aadhaar e-KYC is instant. V-KYC takes 10-15 minutes. In-person verification: 1-2 days.

**Q: Can I complete KYC online without visiting branch?**
A: Yes, through Video KYC or Aadhaar e-KYC.

**Q: What happens if I don't have PAN?**
A: You can submit Form 60, but account opening amount limited to ₹50,000.
