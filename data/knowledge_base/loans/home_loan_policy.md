## Overview

Home loans (also called housing loans or mortgage loans) are secured loans provided for purchasing, constructing, or renovating residential property. This policy outlines eligibility, loan amounts, interest rates, property requirements, and terms.

---

## Loan Purpose Categories

### 1. Home Purchase Loan
- For buying ready-to-move-in flats, apartments, or independent houses
- Maximum LTV (Loan-to-Value): 90% of property value
- Loan amount: ₹10 lakh to ₹5 crore

### 2. Home Construction Loan
- For constructing house on owned land
- Disbursed in stages based on construction progress
- Maximum LTV: 80% of total project cost
- Loan amount: ₹10 lakh to ₹3 crore

### 3. Plot + Construction Loan
- Combined loan for purchasing plot and constructing house
- Plot cost + construction cost covered
- Maximum LTV: 75%
- Loan amount: ₹15 lakh to ₹3 crore

### 4. Home Extension/Renovation Loan
- For adding rooms, floors, or renovating existing property
- Property must be owned by applicant
- Maximum LTV: 70% of current property value
- Loan amount: ₹5 lakh to ₹50 lakh

### 5. Balance Transfer
- Transfer existing home loan from another lender
- Competitive interest rates
- Top-up facility available
- Processing fee waived during promotional periods

## Eligibility Criteria

### For Salaried Individuals

**Age:**
- Minimum: 21 years (or 23 if self-employed)
- Maximum: 65 years at loan maturity (70 for government employees)

**Employment:**
- Minimum 2 years of continuous employment
- At least 1 year with current employer

**Income:**
- Minimum monthly income: ₹30,000 (may vary by city)
- Income stability and growth trajectory considered

**Credit Score:**
- Minimum: 700
- Score 750+: Best interest rates

---

### For Self-Employed

**Age:** 25-70 years at loan maturity

**Business Vintage:** Minimum 3 years of continuous business

**Income:**
- Minimum annual income: ₹6 lakh
- Assessed via ITR and audited financials

**Credit Score:** Minimum 725

## Loan Amount and LTV

### Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios

| Property Value | Maximum LTV |
|----------------|-------------|
| Up to ₹30 lakh | 90% |
| ₹30 lakh - ₹75 lakh | 80% |
| Above ₹75 lakh | 75% |

**Margin/Down Payment:** Customer contributes remaining percentage

**Example:**
- Property value: ₹50 lakh
- LTV: 80%
- Max loan: ₹40 lakh
- Down payment required: ₹10 lakh

### Maximum Loan Amount
- Depends on: Income, age, property value, existing liabilities
- General rule: EMI should not exceed 50% of gross monthly income

## Loan Tenure

**Minimum:** 5 years  
**Maximum:** 30 years  
**Typical:** 15-20 years

**Factors Affecting Tenure:**
- Age of applicant
- Income and repayment capacity
- Property age (older properties may have shorter tenure)

---

## Fees and Charges

### Processing Fee
- 0.50% of loan amount + GST
- Minimum: ₹10,000
- Maximum: ₹50,000
- **Waived** during special campaigns or for premium customers

### Other Charges
- **Legal and Technical Valuation:** ₹5,000 - ₹10,000
- **Documentation Charges:** ₹2,500 + GST
- **Prepayment Charges:** Nil for floating rate loans / 2-4% for fixed rate
- **Late Payment Penalty:** 2% per month on overdue EMI
- **Loan Cancellation:** ₹5,000 (if cancelled post-sanction)
- **CERSAI Charges:** As applicable
- **Stamp Duty and Registration:** Borne by customer

## Required Documentation

### For Salaried
1. **Identity & Address Proof:** Aadhaar, PAN, Passport
2. **Income Proof:**
   - Last 3 months' salary slips
   - Last 6 months' bank statements
   - Form 16 for last 2 years
3. **Employment Proof:** Employment letter, ID card
4. **Property Documents:**
   - Sale agreement
   - Allotment letter
   - Approved building plan
   - NOC from builder/society
   - Property tax receipts
   - Encumbrance certificate

### For Self-Employed
1. **Identity & Address Proof:** Same as salaried
2. **Business Proof:**
   - Business registration
   - GST registration
   - Partnership deed / MOA & AOA
3. **Financial Documents:**
   - ITR for last 3 years
   - Audited financials (last 2 years)
   - Last 12 months' bank statements
4. **Property Documents:** Same as salaried

## Property Eligibility

### Approved Properties
- Residential apartments in approved projects
- Independent houses
- Villas in gated communities
- Builder flats with clear title
- Resale properties with clear ownership

### Property Requirements
- Clear and marketable title
- Approved by local municipal authority
- Completion certificate (for ready properties)
- Occupancy certificate
- Khata/property tax records
- No legal disputes or encumbrances

### Property Types NOT Eligible
- Properties in unauthorized layouts
- Agricultural land
- Properties with disputed ownership
- Properties in litigation
- Very old properties (> 40 years at loan maturity)

## Loan Disbursement

### For Ready Properties (Purchase)
- **Single Disbursement:** Full amount directly to seller/builder
- **Post-Completion:** After registration and possession
- **Timeline:** 7-15 days after documentation

### For Under-Construction Properties
- **Stage-wise Disbursement:** As per construction milestones
- **Typical Stages:**
  1. 10% - Booking/allotment
  2. 20% - Foundation completion
  3. 30% - Plinth level
  4. 20% - Roof/slab completion
  5. 20% - Final completion
- **Pre-EMI:** Interest-only payments during construction
- **Full EMI:** Starts after final disbursement

### For Construction on Owned Land
- **Disbursement in 3-4 stages** linked to construction progress
- Architect/engineer certification required for each stage
- Site visit and inspection by bank's empaneled valuers

## Repayment Options

### Standard EMI
- Fixed monthly installment (Principal + Interest)
- Auto-debit from savings/current account

### Step-Up EMI
- Lower EMIs initially, increasing gradually
- Suitable for young professionals with expected income growth

### Step-Down EMI
- Higher EMIs initially, decreasing later
- Suitable for those nearing retirement

### Flexible EMI
- Option to pay higher amount when surplus available
- Reduces principal and interest burden

## Tax Benefits

### Under Income Tax Act

**Section 80C:** Deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh on principal repayment

**Section 24(b):** Deduction up to ₹2 lakh on interest paid (self-occupied property)

**Section 80EE:** Additional ₹50,000 deduction on interest (first-time buyers, loan up to ₹35 lakh)

*Consult tax advisor for detailed guidance*

## Insurance Requirements

### Property Insurance (Mandatory)
- Fire and allied perils insurance
- Sum insured = Loan amount or property value (whichever is higher)
- Bank assigned as first loss payee
- Premium paid annually

### Term Life Insurance (Highly Recommended)
- Covers outstanding loan in case death of borrower
- Sum assured = Loan amount
- Reduces financial burden on family

---

## Co-Applicant and Guarantor

### Co-Applicant Benefits
- Increases eligibility (combined income considered)
- Can be spouse, parents, or adult children
- Co-applicant becomes co-owner (usually)
- Joint tax benefits available

### Guarantor Requirement
- Required for self-employed applicants in certain cases
- Guarantor's income also assessed
- Guarantor liable if primary borrower defaults

## Special Schemes

### Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)
- Interest subsidy for eligible customers
- Lower income groups: Up to ₹2.67 lakh subsidy
- Check eligibility: Income limits, property value limits, first-time homebuyer
- Bank assists in PMAY application

### Rural Housing Loan
- Special rates for properties in rural/semi-urban areas
- Simplified documentation
- Government subsidy schemes applicable

## Balance Transfer and Top-Up

### Balance Transfer
- Transfer existing loan from other banks
- Benefit from lower interest rates
- Processing fee may be waived

### Top-Up Loan
- Additional loan on existing home loan
- Eligibility: 12+ EMIs paid, good repayment track
- Top-up can be 20-50% of original loan
- For any purpose (renovation, education, medical, etc.)

---

## Prepayment and Foreclosure

**Floating Rate Loans:**
- No prepayment penalty (as per RBI guidelines)
- Can prepay anytime without charges

**Fixed Rate Loans:**
- Prepayment charges: 2-4% of outstanding principal
- Applicable for initial fixed period only

**Part-Prepayment:**
- Minimum amount: ₹50,000
- Can reduce tenure or EMI

---

## Default and Legal Recourse

### Consequences of Default
- Penal interest charged
- Negative impact on credit score
- Legal notice issued after 90 days of default
- Property may be seized and auctioned (SARFAESI Act)

### One-Time Settlement (OTS)
- Available in case of genuine hardship
- Applicant must demonstrate inability to pay
- Settlement at reduced principal (bank discretion)

## FAQs

**Q: Can I get 100% loan?**
A: No, maximum LTV is 90%. You need to contribute at least 10% down payment.

**Q: Can I take joint loan with spouse?**
A: Yes. Combined income considered, increasing eligibility.

**Q: What if I change job during loan tenure?**
A: Inform bank immediately. Loan continues if EMIs are regular.

**Q: Can I rent out the property?**
A: Yes, but inform the bank. Tax benefits may be affected.
