## Overview

This document covers loan modification options including tenure extension, EMI reduction, loan restructuring during financial hardship, prepayment, foreclosure, and final loan closure procedures.

---

## Loan Restructuring Options

### 1. Tenure Extension

**Purpose:** Reduce EMI burden by extending loan tenure

**Eligibility:**
- Loan operational for minimum 12 months
- No defaults in last 6 months
- Good payment track record

**Process:**
1. Submit request via branch or online
2. Revised repayment schedule generated
3. One-time restructuring fee: ₹1,000 + GST
4. New EMI effective from next month

**Impact:**
- Lower monthly EMI
- Higher total interest payout
- Extended repayment period

**Example:**
- Outstanding: ₹5 lakh, Current EMI: ₹12,000 (3 years remaining)
- Extended tenure: 5 years
- New EMI: ₹9,000 (reduced by ₹3,000)

---

### 2. EMI Reduction (Without Tenure Change)

**Purpose:** Reduce EMI amount temporarily

**Eligibility:**
- Available for home loans and auto loans
- Minimum 24 EMIs paid
- No defaults

**Process:**
- Request moratorium or EMI holiday
- Interest continues to accrue
- Unpaid interest added to principal
- EMI resumes after moratorium period
- Tenure extended automatically

**Moratorium Period:** 3-6 months (maximum)
**Charges:** ₹500 processing fee

---

### 3. Step-Up EMI Plan

**Purpose:** Start with lower EMIs, increase gradually

**Eligibility:**
- Young professionals with expected income growth
- Available for personal and home loans

**Structure:**
- Year 1-2: Lower EMI
- Year 3-4: Medium EMI
- Year 5+: Higher EMI

**Benefit:** Improves affordability in initial years

---

### 4. Loan Consolidation

**Purpose:** Merge multiple loans into single loan

**Eligibility:**
- Multiple existing loans with bank
- Good repayment track record
- Credit score > 700

**Process:**
- All loans closed and replaced with single consolidated loan
- Single EMI instead of multiple EMIs
- Potentially lower interest rate
- Extended tenure option

**Charges:** 0.5% of consolidated amount + GST

## Financial Hardship Restructuring

### COVID-19 / Natural Disaster Relief (When Applicable)

**Temporary Relief Measures:**
- EMI moratorium (interest accrual continues)
- Tenure extension without penalty
- Waiver of penal interest
- No negative credit bureau reporting during moratorium

**Eligibility:** As per RBI/government guidelines

---

### Job Loss / Income Reduction

**Request Process:**
1. Inform bank immediately
2. Submit proof of job loss or income reduction
3. Request restructuring meeting
4. Bank assesses repayment capacity

**Possible Solutions:**
- Temporary EMI reduction
- Tenure extension
- Interest-only payments for 3-6 months
- One-Time Settlement (in extreme cases)

**Note:** Proactive communication with bank is critical

## Prepayment (Part Payment)

### Eligibility
- Minimum 6 EMIs paid (for most loans)
- Minimum part payment: ₹25,000 (personal loan), ₹50,000 (home/auto loan)

### Process
1. Login to internet banking or mobile app
2. Select "Prepayment" option
3. Enter amount to prepay
4. Choose: Reduce tenure OR Reduce EMI
5. Confirm payment
6. Revised schedule generated immediately

### Charges
- **Floating Rate Loans:** No charges (as per RBI guidelines)
- **Fixed Rate Loans:** 2-4% of prepayment amount (if within 12 months)

**Tax Benefit:** Prepayment eligible for deduction under Section 80C (home loans)

## Foreclosure (Full Repayment)

### What is Foreclosure?
- Closing loan before completion of tenure
- Paying entire outstanding principal in one go

### Process
1. Request foreclosure statement from bank
2. Statement shows: Outstanding principal, interest till date, foreclosure charges
3. Valid for 7 days
4. Pay amount via NEFT/RTGS or branch
5. Loan account closed within 7 days
6. NOC issued

### Foreclosure Charges

| Loan Type | Floating Rate | Fixed Rate |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| Personal Loan | 4% of outstanding | 5% of outstanding |
| Auto Loan | 4% of outstanding | 5% of outstanding |
| Home Loan | NIL | 2% (if within lock-in) |

**RBI Guideline:** No foreclosure charges on floating rate loans

### Documents Issued Post-Foreclosure
- **No Objection Certificate (NOC)**
- **Loan Closure Letter**
- **Original documents** (for secured loans):
  - Property documents (home loan)
  - Vehicle RC (auto loan)

**Timeline:** NOC and documents within 15 days of final payment

## Loan Transfer (Balance Transfer)

### What is Balance Transfer?
- Transferring outstanding loan from another bank to our bank
- Benefit from lower interest rates
- Reduce EMI or tenure

### Eligibility
- Minimum 12 EMIs paid to existing lender
- Good credit score (> 700)
- No defaults in current loan

### Process
1. Apply for balance transfer with us
2. Provide existing loan details and statements
3. We assess eligibility and offer rate
4. If accepted, we pay off existing lender
5. Loan transferred to our bank
6. New EMI and tenure as per agreement

### Top-Up Option
- Additional loan amount on top of transfer
- For any purpose (not restricted)
- Single EMI for combined amount

**Charges:**
- Processing fee: 1% of transfer amount (₹5,000 - ₹25,000)
- Often waived during promotional periods

## Regular Loan Closure (Tenure Completion)

### Final EMI Payment
- Last EMI paid on due date
- Account auto-closes within 7 days
- SMS/email notification sent

### Post-Closure Documentation
- **Loan closure certificate**
- **NOC (for secured loans)**
- **Original documents returned:**
  - Home loan: Property documents
  - Auto loan: Vehicle RC
  - CERSAI charge removal (home loans)

**Timeline:** 15-21 days from final EMI

### Responsibility After Closure
- **Home Loan:** Remove mortgage entry from property records at Sub-Registrar office (customer's responsibility)
- **Auto Loan:** Remove hypothecation from RC at RTO (bank provides NOC, customer completes formality)

## Missed EMI and Consequences

### Grace Period
- 3 days grace period after due date
- No penalty if paid within grace period

### After Grace Period
-  **Penal Interest:** 2% per month on overdue amount
- **Late Payment Fee:** ₹500 - ₹1,500 (depending on loan amount)
- **Credit Score Impact:** Reported to CIBIL after 30 days overdue

### After 90 Days (NPA Classification)
- Loan classified as Non-Performing Asset
- Legal notice issued
- Serious impact on credit score
- For secured loans: Asset (property/vehicle) may be seized

**Action:** Contact bank immediately if unable to pay EMI. Avoid ignoring reminders.

## One-Time Settlement (OTS)

### What is OTS?
- Settlement of loan at reduced amount
- Offered in genuine financial hardship cases
- Last resort before legal action

### Eligibility
- Proven inability to pay (job loss, medical emergency, business failure)
- Loan already in default (NPA)
- Customer demonstrates willingness to settle

### Process
1. Submit hardship documentation
2. Bank assesses case
3. OTS offer made (typically 60-85% of outstanding)
4. Customer pays lump sum as per offer
5. Loan closed

**Impact on Credit Score:**
- OTS marked as "Settled" in credit report
- Negative impact on future credit applications
- Better than complete default, but still adverse

## Switching Between Fixed and Floating Rate

### Conversion Option
- Switch from fixed to floating or vice versa
- Available once during loan tenure (for home loans)

**Process:**
1. Submit rate conversion request
2. Bank provides revised terms
3. Conversion fee: 0.5% of outstanding principal
4. New rate effective from next EMI

**When to Switch:**
- Fixed to Floating: When market rates are falling
- Floating to Fixed: When rates expected to rise

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I prepay my loan anytime?**
A: Yes, for floating rate loans (no charges). Fixed rate loans may have charges if prepaid within 12 months.

**Q: Will foreclosure affect my credit score?**
A: No, foreclosure improves your credit score as it shows loan repaid successfully.

**Q: Can I increase my EMI to close loan faster?**
A: Yes, you can make part prepayments anytime to reduce principal.

**Q: What happens if I miss one EMI due to oversight?**
A: Pay immediately with late charges. Won't severely impact credit if it's one-time and rectified quickly.

**Q: Can I transfer loan if I change jobs?**
A: Yes, loan continues. Inform bank of job change. EMI should continue uninterrupted.

**Q: I want to sell my mortgaged property. What's the process?**
A: Buyer can pay bank directly to close loan, or you close loan first and then sell.
