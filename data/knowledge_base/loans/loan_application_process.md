## Overview

This document outlines the end-to-end loan application process for personal loans, home loans, auto loans, and other retail loan products. It covers application submission, document verification, credit assessment, sanction, and disbursement stages.

---

## Application Channels

### 1. Online Application
- Website: www.bank.com/apply-loan
- Mobile App: "BankApp" - Loan section
- Advantages: 24/7 access, instant acknowledgement, document upload facility
- Processing Time: Faster (24-48 hours)

### 2. Branch Application
- Visit nearest branch
- Relationship Manager assistance
- Advantages: Personalized guidance, instant document verification
- Processing Time: 3-7 working days

### 3. Phone Banking
- Call loan helpline: 1800-XXX-LOAN
- Request call-back from loan officer
- Application form sent via email/courier

### 4. DSA (Direct Selling Agent)
- Authorized bank partners
- Doorstep service
- Application submitted on customer's behalf

## Stage 2: Application Submission

### Information Required

**Personal Information:**
- Full name, date of birth, gender
- PAN, Aadhaar, contact details
- Current and permanent address
- Marital status, dependents

**Employment/Business Information:**
- Occupation type (salaried/self-employed)
- Employer name and address
- Designation, work experience
- Monthly/annual income

**Loan Details:**
- Loan type and amount
- Preferred tenure
- Purpose of loan
- Existing loans and liabilities

**References:**
- 2 personal references (non-relatives)
- Name, address, phone number

### Document Upload (Online) or Submission (Branch)
- Identity proof
- Address proof
- Income proof
- Employment/business proof
- Bank statements
- Photographs

**Acknowledgement:**
- Application reference number issued
- SMS and email confirmation sent
- Track application status online

---

## Stage 3: Initial Screening

### KYC Verification (1-2 days)
- Verify identity documents against government databases
- Aadhaar authentication (if applicable)
- PAN verification
- Address verification

### Completeness Check
- Ensure all mandatory documents received
- Check for missing information
- Contact customer if additional documents needed

**Outcome:**
- Application proceeds to next stage OR
- Returned for incomplete documentation

---

## Stage 4: Employment and Income Verification

### For Salaried Employees
- **Salary slip verification** (confirm employer details match)
- **Bank statement analysis** (check salary credits, regularity)
- **Employment verification** call to HR department
- **Form 16 / ITR** cross-check

**Red Flags:**
- Irregular salary credits
- Employer not traceable
- Salary account with frequent overdrafts

---

### For Self-Employed
- **ITR verification** (filed with income tax department)
- **GST returns** (if applicable)
- **Bank statement analysis** (business inflows/outflows)
- **Business premises verification** (for loans above certain threshold)
- **Financial audit** review

**Red Flags:**
- Significant difference between declared and ITR income
- Negative cash flows
- High business volatility

**Timeline:** 2-3 days

---

## Stage 5: Credit Bureau Check

### CIBIL / Experian / Equifax Report
- Credit score retrieved
- Credit history analyzed (last 24-36 months)
- Existing loans and credit cards reviewed
- Payment track record assessed

### Credit Score Interpretation
- **750+:** Excellent - Approved at best rates
- **700-749:** Good - Approved at standard rates
- **650-699:** Fair - May require co-applicant or collateral
- **Below 650:** Poor - High risk of rejection

### Red Flags
- Defaults or late payments
- High credit utilization
- Multiple loan enquiries in short period
- Settled accounts (written-off)
- Court judgments or bankruptcy

**Timeline:** 1 day (instant bureau pull)

---

## Stage 6: Credit Assessment and Underwriting

### Income Assessment
- Verify declared income
- Calculate net disposable income
- Assess income stability and growth

### Debt-to-Income (DTI) Calculation
- Total monthly EMI obligations / Gross monthly income
- Maximum allowed DTI: 50% (some cases 60%)

**Example:**
- Gross monthly income: ₹80,000
- Existing EMIs: ₹20,000
- Proposed loan EMI: ₹18,000
- Total EMI: ₹38,000
- DTI: 47.5% ✅ Acceptable

### Loan-to-Value (LTV) Check (for secured loans)
- Property/vehicle valuation
- Ensure LTV within policy limits
- Adequate margin/down payment

### Risk Categorization
- Low Risk: High credit score, stable income, low DTI
- Medium Risk: Average credit score, moderate DTI
- High Risk: Low credit score, high DTI, unstable income

**Decision:**
- Loan approved with proposed amount and terms OR
- Loan approved with reduced amount / higher rate OR
- Additional conditions (co-applicant, collateral) OR
- Rejected

**Timeline:** 2-4 days

---

## Stage 7: Sanction and Offer

### Sanction Letter Issued
- Loan amount approved
- Interest rate
- Tenure
- EMI amount
- Processing fees and charges
- Validity: 6 months (can be extended)

**Communicated via:**
- Email
- SMS
- Physical letter (if requested)
- Available in mobile app/internet banking

### Customer Acceptance
- Customer reviews sanction letter
- Accepts terms and conditions
- Confirms intent to proceed
- Pays processing fee (if not deducted from loan)

**If Customer Declines:**
- Application cancelled
- Processing fee refunded (if applicable as per policy)

**Timeline:** 1 day

---

## Stage 8: Legal and Technical Verification (For Secured Loans)

### For Home Loans
- **Title verification** by bank's legal team
- **Property valuation** by empaneled valuers
- Check for encumbrances, legal disputes
- Verify building approvals and compliance

### For Auto Loans
- **Vehicle valuation** (if used vehicle)
- Verify dealer authorization (if new vehicle)
- Check vehicle documents (RC, insurance)

**Timeline:** 5-10 days (home loans), 1-2 days (auto loans)

---

## Stage 9: Loan Documentation

### Loan Agreement Signing
- Loan agreement prepared
- Customer visits branch (or doorstep service for premium customers)
- Agreement executed on stamp paper
- Borrower and co-applicant signatures
- Witness signatures

**Documents Signed:**
- Loan agreement
- Repayment schedule
- Hypothecation deed (vehicles) / Mortgage deed (property)
- EMI mandate / Standing instruction form
- Insurance assignment
- Declaration and undertaking

**For Home Loans:**
- Mortgage deed registration at Sub-Registrar office
- Stamp duty and registration charges borne by customer

**Timeline:** 1-2 days

---

## Stage 10: Disbursement

### Pre-Disbursement Checklist
- ✅ All documents signed
- ✅ Processing fee received
- ✅ Insurance purchased and assigned
- ✅ Post-dated cheques (if applicable) or EMI mandate set up
- ✅ Hypothecation/mortgage registered

### Disbursement Methods

**Personal Loan:**
- Credited to customer's savings/current account
- Instant credit (within hours of documentation)

**Home Loan:**
- Direct payment to builder/seller
- Account payee cheque or RTGS
- Disbursed after registration

**Auto Loan:**
- Payment to dealer or seller
- Customer pays balance (down payment + registration)

**Construction/Renovation Loan:**
- Staged disbursement based on milestones
- Certificate from approved architect/engineer required for each stage

**Disbursement Confirmation:**
- SMS and email notification
- Disbursement letter issued
- Repayment schedule provided

**Timeline:** Same day to 7 days (depends on loan type)

---

## Stage 11: EMI Commencement

### First EMI Due
- **Personal/Auto Loan:** 30 days from disbursement
- **Home Loan (ready property):** 30 days from disbursement
- **Home Loan (under construction):** Pre-EMI (interest-only) during construction, full EMI after final disbursement

### EMI Auto-Debit Setup
- Standing instruction on savings/current account
- EMI deducted automatically on due date
- Sufficient balance must be maintained

### EMI Schedule
- Provided at disbursement
- Shows month-wise principal and interest breakup
- Available in mobile app/internet banking

**Reminder:**
- SMS sent 3 days before EMI due date
- Email notification

## Reasons for Rejection

**Common Reasons:**
- Low credit score
- Insufficient income
- High debt-to-income ratio
- Incomplete/fraudulent documentation
- Negative employment verification
- Property/vehicle not meeting eligibility criteria
- Adverse credit history

**Communication:**
- Rejection communicated via email/SMS
- Reason mentioned (if permissible)
- Suggest corrective actions

**Re-Application:**
- Allowed after 3-6 months
- After addressing rejection reasons
- Improve credit score, reduce existing debt, increase income
