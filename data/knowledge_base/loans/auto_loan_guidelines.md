## Overview

Auto loans are provided for purchasing new or used vehicles including cars, SUVs, commercial vehicles, and two-wheelers. This document covers eligibility, loan amounts, interest rates, tenure, and documentation requirements.

---

## Vehicle Categories

### 1. New Car Loan
- For brand-new cars from authorized dealers
- Maximum LTV: 90% of on-road price
- Loan amount: ₹1 lakh to ₹75 lakh
- Tenure: Up to 7 years

### 2. Used Car Loan
- For pre-owned cars (up to 5 years old)
- Maximum LTV: 80% of current market value
- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹25 lakh
- Tenure: Up to 5 years
- Vehicle condition and valuation assessment required

### 3. Two-Wheeler Loan
- For motorcycles and scooters (new and used)
- Maximum LTV: 90% (new), 80% (used)
- Loan amount: ₹25,000 to ₹5 lakh
- Tenure: Up to 5 years

### 4. Commercial Vehicle Loan
- For trucks, taxis, auto-rickshaws (new and used)
- Maximum LTV: 75%
- Loan amount: ₹2 lakh to ₹50 lakh
- Tenure: Up to 7 years

## Processing Fees and Charges

### One-Time Fees
- **Processing Fee:** 1.5% - 2% of loan amount + GST (minimum ₹2,500)
- **Documentation:** ₹500 + GST
- **Vehicle Valuation (Used):** ₹1,000 - ₹2,500

### Other Charges
- **Prepayment:** 3-5% of outstanding principal (if prepaid within 12 months)
- **Foreclosure:** 4% of outstanding principal
- **Late Payment:** 2% per month on overdue EMI + ₹500 fixed penalty
- **EMI Bounce:** ₹750 per instance
- **Loan Cancellation:** ₹3,000 (if cancelled post sanction)

**Waiver:** Processing fee waived for premium customers or during promotional periods

## Required Documentation

### Common Documents (All Categories)
1. **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, DL
2. **Address Proof:** Utility bill, Aadhaar, Passport
3. **Age Proof:** Aadhaar, Birth certificate, PAN
4. **Income Proof:**
   - Salaried: Salary slips (3 months), bank statement (6 months), Form 16
   - Self-Employed: ITR (2 years), bank statement (12 months), financials
5. **Photographs:** 2 passport-size

### Vehicle Documents
- **New Vehicle:**
  - Proforma invoice from dealer
  - Quotation showing on-road price
  - Dealer registration and authorization
  
- **Used Vehicle:**
  - Original Registration Certificate (RC)
  - Insurance copy (valid minimum 6 months remaining)
  - NOC from previous financier (if applicable)
  - Vehicle service history
  - RTO transfer forms

## Vehicle Insurance Requirements

### Comprehensive Insurance (Mandatory)
- Covers own damage + third-party liability
- Must be valid throughout loan tenure
- Bank assigned as hypothecation holder
- Sum insured = Loan amount or vehicle IDV (whichever is higher)

### Add-on Covers (Recommended)
- Zero depreciation cover
- Engine protection
- Return to invoice (RTI) cover
- Roadside assistance

**Premium Payment:**
- Can be included in loan amount (up to ₹50,000)
- Or paid separately by customer

**Renewal:** Customer responsible for timely renewal. Bank sends reminders.

## Loan Disbursement Process

### For New Vehicles
1. Loan sanctioned based on proforma invoice
2. Amount directly paid to dealer
3. Customer pays down payment + registration to dealer
4. Vehicle registered in customer's name with bank hypothecation
5. RC submitted to bank
6. EMI starts from next month

### For Used Vehicles
1. Vehicle inspected and valued by bank's empaneled agency
2. Loan amount based on valuation report
3. Amount paid to seller (through account payee cheque)
4. Ownership transferred to buyer
5. Hypothecation registered in favor of bank
6. RC submitted to bank

**Timeline:** 3-5 working days from documentation

---

## Hypothecation and RC

**Hypothecation:**
- Vehicle serves as collateral
- Bank's lien marked on RC
- Customer cannot sell without NOC

**RC Custody:**
- Original RC submitted to bank
- Returned after full loan repayment
- NOC issued for hypothecation removal

**Violation:**
- Selling vehicle without NOC is illegal
- Bank has right to seize vehicle

## Repayment and EMI

### EMI Structure
- Equated Monthly Installment (Principal + Interest)
- Auto-debit from savings/current account
- EMI date: Can choose (1st, 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th, 25th)

### EMI Calculation Example
- Loan: ₹5 lakh
- Interest: 9% p.a.
- Tenure: 5 years
- **EMI: ~₹10,381**
- Total payment: ₹6.23 lakh (interest: ₹1.23 lakh)

### Prepayment
- Allowed anytime after 6 EMIs
- Part prepayment minimum: ₹25,000
- Reduces tenure or EMI (customer choice)
- Charges apply if prepaid within 12 months

## Exchange Benefit (Cash Discount)

**For New Vehicles:**
- Bank tie-ups with dealers for exchange offers
- Old vehicle exchanged for new one
- Exchange value adjusted in down payment
- Increases eligibility for loan

**Example:**
- New car on-road price: ₹8 lakh
- Exchange value of old car: ₹2 lakh
- Net cost: ₹6 lakh
- Down payment (10%): ₹60,000 (adjusted from exchange)

## Loan Transfer and Foreclosure

### Balance Transfer
- Transfer loan from another bank/NBFC
- Benefit from lower interest rates
- Outstanding amount transferred to our bank
- Vehicle re-hypothecated

### Foreclosure
- Close loan before tenure completion
- Rs ₹4% charges if closed within 12 months
- No charges after 12 months
- RC and NOC issued within 7 days

## Default and Consequences

### What Happens on EMI Default
- Late payment penalty charged
- Negative impact on credit score
- Reminder calls and notices
- After 90 days: Legal notice
- After 120 days: Vehicle seizure proceedings

### Vehicle Repossession
- Bank has right to seize vehicle under contract
- Vehicle auctioned to recover dues
- Shortfall (if any) still payable by customer

**Avoid Default:** Contact bank immediately if facing repayment difficulty. Loan restructuring may be possible.

## Used Vehicle Valuation

### Valuation Process
1. Bank empanels authorized valuers
2. Physical inspection of vehicle
3. Check engine, transmission, body condition
4. Verify odometer reading
5. Check service history
6. Market comparison for model/year
7. Valuation report issued (valid 30 days)

**Valuation Charges:** ₹1,000 - ₹2,500 (borne by customer)

**Eligibility Factors:**
- Vehicle age (max 5 years for loan approval)
- Total age at maturity (not more than 10 years)
- Kilometers driven (lower is better)
- Model popularity and resale value

## FAQs

**Q: Can I get 100% loan for a vehicle?**
A: No, maximum is 90%. Minimum 10% down payment required.

**Q: Can I take loan for vehicle registered in another city?**
A: Yes, but vehicle must be re-registered in your city and hypothecated to our bank.

**Q: What if I damage the vehicle?**
A: Insurance covers repair. Inform bank if damage is major. Loan repayment continues.

**Q: Can I sell the vehicle before loan closure?**
A: Yes, but you must get NOC from bank. Either close loan first, or buyer pays bank directly.

**Q: Is vehicle inspection required for new vehicles?**
A: No, only for used vehicles.
