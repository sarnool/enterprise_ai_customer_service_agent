## Overview

Personal loans are unsecured loans provided for various personal needs such as medical emergencies, weddings, education, travel, debt consolidation, or home renovation. This document outlines eligibility criteria, loan amounts, interest rates, tenure options, and terms.

---

## Loan Amounts and Tenure

### Loan Amount
- **Minimum:** ₹50,000
- **Maximum:** ₹25,00,000
- **Amount depends on:** Income, credit score, existing obligations, employment stability

### Loan Tenure
- **Minimum:** 12 months (1 year)
- **Maximum:** 60 months (5 years)
- Longer tenure = Lower EMI, Higher total interest
- Shorter tenure = Higher EMI, Lower total interest

---

## Eligibility Criteria

### For Salaried Employees

**Age:**
- Minimum: 21 years at time of application
- Maximum: 60 years at loan maturity

**Employment:**
- Minimum 2 years of work experience
- At least 1 year with current employer
- Employed with reputed organization (government, MNC, listed company, or established private firm)

**Income:**
- Minimum monthly income: ₹25,000 (metro cities)
- Minimum monthly income: ₹20,000 (non-metro)
- Income stability is critical (consistent salary credits)

**Credit Score:**
- Minimum CIBIL score: 700
- Score 750+: Preferred rate of interest
- Score below 700: May require co-applicant or higher rate

---

### For Self-Employed Individuals

**Age:**
- Minimum: 25 years
- Maximum: 65 years at loan maturity

**Business Vintage:**
- Minimum 3 years in current business
- Business should be profitable for at least 2 consecutive years

**Income:**
- Minimum annual income: ₹4,00,000
- Income assessed based on ITR and bank statements

**Credit Score:**
- Minimum: 725
- Higher score improves approval chances

**Business Type:**
- Proprietorship, partnership, or private limited company
- Valid business registration and GST (if applicable)

## Processing Fees and Charges

### One-Time Charges
- **Processing Fee:** 2% of loan amount + GST (minimum ₹1,000, maximum ₹15,000)
- **Documentation Charges:** ₹500 + GST
- **Verification Charges:** ₹500 (for employment/income verification)

### Waiver on Processing Fee
- Processing fee waived for:
  - Premium banking customers
  - Loans above ₹10 lakh (discretionary)
  - Special promotional campaigns

### Other Charges
- **Prepayment Charges:** 4% of outstanding principal (if prepaid before 12 months)
- **Foreclosure Charges:** 5% of outstanding principal
- **Late Payment Penalty:** 2% per month on overdue EMI
- **Cheque Bounce Charges:** ₹750 per instance
- **EMI/NACH bounce:** ₹500 per instance

## Loan-to-Income Ratio

**Debt-to-Income (DTI) Guidelines:**
- Total EMI obligations (including proposed loan) should not exceed **50% of gross monthly income**
- For income > ₹1 lakh per month, DTI can go up to 60%

**Example:**
- Monthly income: ₹60,000
- Existing EMIs: ₹15,000
- Available for new EMI: ₹15,000 (50% of ₹60,000 - ₹15,000 existing)
- Maximum loan eligibility at 12% for 5 years: ~₹10 lakh

## Required Documentation

### For Salaried Employees
1. **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID
2. **Address Proof:** Utility bill, Aadhaar, Passport, Rent agreement
3. **Income Proof:**
   - Last 3 months' salary slips
   - Last 6 months' bank statement
   - Form 16 or ITR (last 2 years)
4. **Employment Proof:**
   - Employment letter or ID card
   - HR contact for verification
5. **Photographs:** 2 recent passport-size

### For Self-Employed
1. **Identity and Address Proof:** Same as salaried
2. **Income Proof:**
   - ITR for last 2-3 years
   - Audited financials (P&L, Balance Sheet)
   - Last 12 months' bank statement
3. **Business Proof:**
   - Business registration certificate
   - GST registration
   - Shop/office address proof
4. **Photographs:** 2 recent passport-size

**Additional for Loans > ₹10 Lakh:**
- Property documents (as collateral, if applicable)
- Co-applicant income proof

## Loan Approval Process

### Step 1: Application Submission
- Online application via website/mobile app
- Visit branch with documents
- Call relationship manager

### Step 2: Document Verification
- KYC verification
- Income and employment verification
- Credit bureau check (CIBIL, Experian, etc.)

### Step 3: Credit Assessment
- Credit score evaluation
- Income stability check
- Existing debt obligations review
- Banking relationship history

### Step 4: Sanction
- Loan sanctioned with approved amount and terms
- Sanction letter issued (valid for 6 months)
- Acceptance of terms by customer

### Step 5: Disbursement
- Loan agreement signing
- Disbursement to customer's account (same day to 48 hours)
- EMI auto-debit setup

**Timeline:** 3-7 working days from complete documentation

## Repayment Options

### EMI (Equated Monthly Installment)
- Fixed EMI throughout tenure
- EMI = Principal + Interest component
- EMI auto-debited from savings/current account on fixed date (1st, 5th, 10th, 15th, or 20th of month)

### Prepayment Options
- Partial prepayment allowed (minimum ₹5,000)
- Full prepayment/foreclosure allowed anytime
- Charges apply if prepaid within 12 months
- No charges on prepayment after 12 months (as per RBI guidelines for floating rate loans)

### EMI Date Change
- Can be changed once during loan tenure
- Request 15 days before current EMI date
- Charges: ₹500

## Special Schemes

### Pre-Approved Personal Loans
- Available to existing customers with good banking history
- Instant approval (no fresh documentation)
- Competitive interest rates
- Disbursal within 24 hours

**Eligibility:**
- Savings/salary account active for 12+ months
- Regular salary credits
- No loan defaults
- Credit score > 750

### Top-Up Loan
- Available to customers with existing personal loan
- Minimum 12 EMIs paid on existing loan
- Top-up amount: Up to 75% of existing loan principal paid
- Same interest rate as existing loan
- Extend tenure or increase EMI

## Rejection Criteria

**Common Reasons for Rejection:**
- Credit score below minimum threshold
- Insufficient income
- High existing debt obligations (DTI > 50%)
- Unstable employment history
- Negative remarks in credit report
- Defaults on previous loans
- Fraudulent documentation

**After Rejection:**
- Communicate reason to applicant (if permissible)
- Suggest corrective actions (improve credit score, reduce debt, increase income)
- Reapply after 3-6 months

## Insurance Options

### Credit Protection Insurance (Optional)
- Covers outstanding loan in case of:
  - Death of borrower
  - Permanent disability
  - Critical illness

**Premium:** 0.5% - 1% of loan amount (one-time or annual)

**Not Mandatory:** Customer can choose to opt-out

## FAQs

**Q: Can I get a loan if I have an existing loan?**
A: Yes, if your DTI is within 50% and you have a good repayment track record.

**Q: Will taking a personal loan affect my credit score?**
A: Yes. Timely repayment improves score; defaults damage it.

**Q: Can I prepay my loan?**
A: Yes. Charges apply if prepaid within 12 months. Free after 12 months.

**Q: What if I miss an EMI?**
A: Late payment penalty applies. Multiple defaults reported to CIBIL, affecting future creditworthiness.

**Q: Can I transfer my loan to another bank?**
A: Yes, balance transfer facility available subject to new bank's approval.
